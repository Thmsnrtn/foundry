# Canonical Corpus Synthesis

## Status and interpretation

This document preserves the substantive V1→V8+ lineage as durable repository context. V1→V8+ is an evolution of understanding, not eight releases to build in sequence. Historical plans and their dependency DAG are planning evidence. The governing target is the latest coherent institution reconciled with current code, constitutional invariants, and verified evidence. Obsolete packages are marked superseded rather than recreated.

## Mission and owner transition

Foundry's target form is an **Adaptive Company Institution**: persistent institutional intelligence that can enter an unfamiliar company, reconstruct how it works, understand owner purpose and constraints, decide across time horizons, operate only under earned authority, verify effects, learn, improve economics, and progressively carry real responsibility.

The intended owner transition is from persistent intelligence layer and operational coordinator toward purpose, boundaries, consequential judgment, and exception ownership. Feature count and agent activity are not substitutes for that transfer.

## Canonical loops

```text
Reality → Evidence → Claim → Judgment / Decision → Authority → ActionPlan
→ Execution → ActionReceipt → Outcome → Learning
```

A model statement is not evidence; capability is not authority; execution is not completion; provider acceptance is not an independently observed outcome.

```text
Baseline → Contract → Build → Challenge → Compare → Observe → Decide
→ Cutover → Delete → Promote evidence maturity
```

Every consequential migration and responsibility promotion remains legible through this proof loop.

## Constitutional synthesis

- Company over platform; adapt to the company rather than forcing a Foundry-shaped process.
- Capability over integration; APIs, browsers, CLIs, MCP, humans, and physical work are replaceable ControlPaths.
- Evidence over narrative; unknown is a legitimate institutional state.
- Responsibility precedes capability; competence never implies authority.
- Consequential mutations cross one governed boundary with trusted identity, policy, deduplication, spend controls, receipts, and reconciliation.
- Preserve request, provider acknowledgment, observed effect, and verified business outcome as distinct states.
- Owner authority is understandable, bounded, revocable, and never expanded by a model, caller, or ControlPath.
- No orphan abstractions, permanent dual-write, competing canonical truth, or compatibility layer without a deletion plan. Deletion is completion.
- Founder-facing simplicity cannot hide uncertainty, authority, or consequence.
- Public claims cannot outrun evidence. Simpler equivalent architecture wins. Every subsystem and cognition step must pay rent.

## Evidence and responsibility

Evidence maturity is exactly **E0 — Hypothesis**, **E1 — Static implemented**, **E2 — Runtime verified**, **E3 — Benchmark proven**, **E4 — Pilot proven**, **E5 — Production proven**, and **E6 — Broad institutional evidence**. Implementation and local tests do not establish production proof.

Responsibility moves only by evidence and owner policy:

```text
Unknown → Visible → Understood → Shadowing → Assisting → Operating
→ Mature → Exception-owned
```

Founder-attention reduction and safe exception ownership are system outcomes.

## Founder experience

The primary operating model is **TODAY | ASK | COMPANY | ACTIVITY | CONTROL**. Internal ontology is translated into plain language without erasing precision. The seven-day owner-absence proof target reports **HANDLED**, **CHANGED**, **NEEDS YOU**, **DELIBERATELY NOT DONE**, and **STILL OPEN**, retaining evidence, effect certainty, responsibility, and deadlines.

## Economics

Optimize maximum defensible company value per unit of money, model/token use, compute, latency, engineering and operational complexity, founder attention, security risk, authority risk, reliability risk, and marginal value—subject to quality, safety, law, contract, and owner policy.

Use the cheapest path demonstrated sufficient:

```text
deterministic/cache/retrieval → inexpensive model → standard model
→ premium reasoning → executive/challenge → human
```

Measure overspend and underspend regret. A plan changes how much Foundry can carry, not whether it attempts to carry responsibilities intelligently. Safety, honesty, and authority are not premium-tier features.

## Company understanding and cognition

For companies not pre-modeled, reconstruct purpose, responsibilities, people and systems, capabilities, dependencies, processes, evidence, risks, economics, commitments, authority, ControlPaths, and open loops. Ontology must demonstrate operational usefulness.

Cognition spans immediate operations, tactical planning, strategy, learning, risk, economics, and owner constraints. Roles and competencies may be dynamic; permanent named executive personas are not the kernel. Expensive deliberation survives only when it demonstrates marginal value over simpler baselines.

## AcreOS and generalization

AcreOS is the golden unfamiliar-company adoption target. Foundry should reconstruct and progressively operate it through general company semantics:

```text
Owner purpose/boundaries/judgment
→ Foundry understanding/strategy/operation/development/verification/learning
→ models/Codex/APIs/humans → AcreOS
```

There are no `if AcreOS` kernel branches. AcreOS facts live in company data or configuration. A primitive revealed by AcreOS enters the kernel only if it generalizes.

## Development and recursive institution

Development is a governed capability:

```text
problem/evidence → requirement → plan → implementation → tests → challenge
→ deployment/cutover → runtime observation → learning
```

It must work for arbitrary software companies, AcreOS, and Foundry. Keep three rings distinct: Foundry the company, Foundry the product, and Foundry the constitution. Foundry may operate and improve Foundry; it may not self-authorize, rewrite its constitution, manipulate its evaluation, or self-certify consequential changes. Independent or frozen evaluation governs recursive work.

## Control, spend, and external effects

Identity, credentials, policy, and ControlPaths are institution-owned. Retry semantics distinguish definitely not executed, definitely executed, and ambiguous. Ambiguity creates reconciliation work rather than optimistic success or blind retry. Material spend uses atomic authorization/reservation under concurrency and records real provider cost where available.

## Macro program

The non-sacred dependency map is: foundational integrity; thin institutional spine; founder operating experience; AcreOS adoption; executive cognition; development institution; recursive institution; responsibility and commitment; zero-training founder UX; generalization; external pilot; production claims; cost truth; quality/cost frontier; recursive self-improvement; and customer ROI/final production gate. Verified risk and dependencies may reorder work. Tranche boundaries are checkpoints, not review gates.

## Explicit non-targets

Absent verified need, do not add permanent named-agent theater, unnecessary dashboards, unrestricted browser or money autonomy, company-specific kernel logic, speculative scale architecture, native clients, ornamental ontology, self-directed constitutional mutation, expensive unmeasured reasoning, duplicate truth, permanent dual-write, or compatibility layers without deletion.

## Completion standard

Overall completion requires whole-system audit of architecture, authority, security, effects, financial invariants, AI spend, evidence, responsibility, founder experience, unfamiliar-company adoption, AcreOS generalization, cognition, development, recursion, economics, migrations, deletion, public claims, and external proof debt, plus the complete deterministic validation suite. Until E4–E6 evidence exists, pilot, production, and broad claims remain explicitly unproven.
