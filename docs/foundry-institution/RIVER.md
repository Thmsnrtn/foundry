# The River

> Canonical destination, condensed from the River constitution (2026-09-07). This document is a design corpus and an economic theory, not a checklist. Every mechanism it names must still earn existence under `ARCHITECTURE.md` (ontology is admitted only when it changes a useful decision, control, execution, accounting truth, risk boundary, or proof). `IMPLEMENTATION_STATE.md` says what is real.

## Three identities

| Identity | What it is | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundry** | The governed autonomous institution. | Evidence semantics, responsibilities, authority, policy, execution governance, economic stewardship, institutional memory, the founder interface. |
| **The River** | The economic ecology Foundry stewards: opportunities, probes, engines, assets, cells, distribution, data, brands, relationships, treasury, and accumulated experience. | Nothing. The River is subject matter, not a component. It is Foundry's economic metabolism. |
| **Fable** | The recursively governed development institution that improves Foundry. | No capital, no authority over the River. It observes repeated institutional friction and improves Foundry when evidence shows Foundry is the bottleneck. |

The three rings in `CONSTITUTION.md` (Foundry the company, the product, the constitution) are unchanged and orthogonal: they describe how Foundry operates itself; these identities describe what Foundry is for.

The recursive loop: the River operates → reality exposes institutional friction → Foundry records evidence → Fable investigates → Fable improves Foundry → capability propagates where proven → future River operation becomes cheaper and better.

## Purpose

Foundry is not primarily an autonomous SaaS operator, an AI workforce, a venture studio, an idea-to-app machine, or a portfolio dashboard. The destination is a private, owner-governed autonomous economic institution that continuously discovers, validates, creates, acquires, operates, compounds, protects, harvests, sells, retires, and regenerates a diversified portfolio of independently useful digital economic assets while progressively reducing owner intervention.

The objective is **maximum durable owner-benefiting economic value per unit of capital, compute, stewardship complexity, founder attention, and risk**, subject to invariant quality, truthfulness, customer-value, legal, authority, and trust floors. It is not maximum businesses, maximum MRR, maximum agent activity, or maximum reinvestment.

Three kinds of wealth: **Flow** (durable surplus and owner-distributable cash), **Asset** (transferable software, domains, brands, data, audiences, rights, relationships), and **Institutional** (Foundry's accumulated ability to create and steward future value). The third may matter most.

**The River serves the owner. The owner does not serve the River.** Owner distribution is a first-class successful disposition of capital.

## The River of Nickels

AI lowers the minimum economically viable size of a business. The deepest frontier is economic friction too small for conventional entrepreneurship but large enough for autonomous entrepreneurship. A Nickel is a small stream of durable profit, not a tiny payment; aggregate small value into economic transactions (annual licenses, bundles, memberships, credits). Scales: Droplets, Streams, Tributaries, Rivers. Growth is optional; a small, durable, near-zero-maintenance engine may stay small indefinitely.

## The economic unit is earned, not assumed

`products` is Foundry's tenancy scope, not its economic unit. Product semantics fit SaaS; they do not fit lead fees, licensing, reports, sponsorship, affiliate revenue, several monetization mechanisms inside one product, or hundreds of lightweight engines. The candidate distinction is **CashFlowEngine**: a repeatable mechanism through which Foundry creates or facilitates a legitimate economic benefit and captures sustainable flow. It is not implemented. Real River pilots pressure Product semantics; if they cannot faithfully represent independent economic mechanisms, Engine has earned ontology. The same rule governs every candidate primitive: DemandSignal, Observation, OpportunityThesis, Probe, ValueEvent, EconomicPrimitive, Asset, RightsRecord, SourceContract, Dependency, CapitalDemand, EconomicCell, Reservoir, VentureSpecies, Capability, PortfolioPolicy, TreasuryPosition, ExperienceRecord.

## Evidence discipline

Models interpret reality; they do not define it. The epistemic states are observed, known, inferred, estimated, experimental, protective, unknown, conflicting, stale, unverified. Unknown is valid. A model-generated probability never silently becomes a fact; repeated copies of one source are not corroboration; a later success story does not rewrite an earlier failed prediction.

**Commercial evidence is separate from implementation proof.** E0–E6 says whether Foundry's machinery is proven. CE0–CE6 says whether a thesis has met commercial reality:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CE0 | Economic hypothesis only |
| CE1 | Real friction or existing spending observed |
| CE2 | Meaningful behavioural commitment |
| CE3 | Settled payment |
| CE4 | Promised ValueEvent delivered |
| CE5 | Repeat, renewal, referral, or repeatable distribution |
| CE6 | Sustained positive contribution under meaningful conditions |

A product can have strong commercial evidence and weak implementation maturity, or the reverse. Never conflate them.

## The Sensorium

Maintain an evidence-backed picture of economically relevant change at a cost justified by the decisions it can improve. Follow revealed economic behaviour before opinion: a complaint is friction; a company paying to solve it is stronger; a settled payment is stronger still. Signals are not opportunities: Raw Signal → Economic Observation → Economic Thesis → Decision-Critical Unknown → Investigation → Probe. Source reliability is learned empirically and contextually. Focused vision covers existing markets, customers, providers, competitors, regulators, sources, distribution, risks; the Horizon covers change classes historically capable of moving the investable frontier, never an endless news reader.

**Rights:** access does not imply the right to commercialize. "Can Foundry access this?" and "Can Foundry commercially use this in the proposed way?" are separate questions. Every meaningful source should eventually express identity, access, provenance, ownership, permitted use and derivation, redistribution, attribution, rate limits, retention, refresh cadence, privacy class, cost, reliability, termination risk, substitutes, and last terms snapshot.

## Decision-critical uncertainty and Probes

Do not maximize research. Ask which uncertainty is most likely to change the next decision, and resolve that one. Research terminates at actionability; a $40 payment probe usually beats four days of market sizing.

A **Probe** is an intentionally disposable economic experiment, the smallest credible encounter with reality that can answer the decision-critical unknown. It specifies hypothesis, participant, ValueEvent, capture, distribution, operation, rights status, budget, horizon, success condition, kill condition, reputation and legal boundaries, authority boundary, and expected information. **Probes are born with kill criteria.** Hindsight rationalization ("no payment, but engagement looked encouraging, spend another $1,000") is refused: the original probe can still fail; a new observation earns a new hypothesis, and both truths are preserved.

Failure is normal. Optimize the **cost of disconfirmation** as much as the probability of correct prediction. Ask how much failure cost, how quickly contradiction was recognized, whether stop rules worked, and what information, assets, and judgment survived. Probes return cash, information, assets, capability, or portfolio understanding; do not pretend all of these convert to dollars.

## Value before monetization

Ask first what real economic benefit occurs because this exists (the **ValueEvent**). Separate four hypotheses that all need support before durable commitment: Value (someone benefits), Capture (Foundry can sustainably collect some of it), Distribution (the participants can be reached economically), Operation (Foundry can deliver reliably at acceptable cost). Distinguish user, beneficiary, payer, and counterparty; charge at the most natural capture point. The economic primitive follows the cadence and location of value: one-time value → sale; refreshed value → subscription; discrete successful event → lead or commission; commercial reuse → license; audience access → sponsorship; variable consumption → usage. Recurring payment must correspond to recurring value.

Capture a sustainable fraction of value and leave customer surplus. Prefer earned retention to trapped retention: clear cancellation, honest pricing, portable data. Trust is capital with deposits and withdrawals; cross-sell respects relevance, consent, frequency, privacy, and expectation. Free is legitimate when it performs an economic job whose downstream value is tracked where attributable. Bundle small value events along real economic adjacency; decompose large products into independently valuable atoms where useful. Productized services are valid probes; automate gradually as repetition appears.

## Lifecycle and the laws of motion

Ambient Reality → Signal → Observation → Thesis → Decision-Critical Unknown → Investigation → Probe Design → Authority Gate → Probe Live → Commercial Evidence → Probe Disposition → Engine Candidate → Incubation → Operating Engine → Crystallization → Asset Formation → Cell Evaluation → Deepening/Replication → Graduation → Scaling → Harvest/Rehabilitation/Sale/Sunset → Salvage → Autopsy → Archive → Possible Resurrection.

Every transition must change what is believed, how much capital is justified, what authority exists, what quality obligations exist, and what the founder needs to see. Transitions are evidence-bearing, never confidence-bearing.

- **Promotion.** Nothing becomes more general, funded, autonomous, shared, or permanent merely because it exists. Signal earns Observation; Observation earns Thesis; Thesis earns Probe; Probe earns Engine; Engine earns capital; responsibility earns authority; local learning earns broader prior; repeated code earns shared capability; Engine earns Product status only if needed; Product or Cell earns legal separation only when economics and risk justify it.
- **Reversibility.** At the earliest stage compatible with learning, commitments stay cheap to reverse: small spend, temporary infrastructure, short commitments, limited brand exposure, minimal data retention, no unnecessary entities, no irreversible engineering.
- **Quiet maturity.** Stable engines are protected from unnecessary improvement. Harvest is a valid state. Doing nothing is often the high-quality action.
- **Salvage.** Before an engine dies, identify what survives (domain, code, dataset, research, relationships, audience, distribution, templates, knowledge) and transfer it.
- **Resurrection requires changed reality.** Rejections are causal ("commercial value supported, but fulfillment economics exceed viable cost at tested pricing"), never a score. When a constraint changes (new API, cheaper compute, new distribution, Fable capability, legal clarity, competitor exit), ask which rejected theses were blocked primarily by it, then form a revised thesis and a new probe. History stays immutable.

## Responsibilities, not agent theater

The durable unit is the responsibility (customer acquisition, pricing, fulfillment, data quality, treasury, opportunity discovery, capital allocation). Cognitive workers are invoked dynamically and disappear; agents are ephemeral labour, the institution remains. Executive reasoning is invoked when competing responsibilities require synthesis; no persistent model identity is the container of authority, memory, truth, or strategy. A named executive interface, if any, is presentation.

Authority follows competence and is granular: scoped by responsibility, consequence, capital, reversibility, and domain. A cell may run $100 probes autonomously and not buy businesses. Authority can regress. Success does not silently expand it; repeated founder approvals do not create standing permission. Foundry may propose a policy; the owner explicitly grants it.

## The capital constitution

Every dollar returns to common Treasury; an engine does not keep the money it produces and competes for the next dollar like any other opportunity. Capital has no loyalty to the asset that produced it. Treasury determines what resources truly exist before allocation begins, in conceptual layers: obligations, survival reserve, operating capital, regeneration capital, scaling capital, institutional capital, opportunity reserve, owner distributable. Ratios are policy; semantics are constitutional.

- **Distribution is success.** Never develop an anti-distribution bias. Cash is an asset; holding cash is valid.
- **Owner modes** (Build, Balance, Income, Preserve, Transition) compile into inspectable policy; natural language is input, deterministic policy is authority.
- **Survival before return.** No ordinary autonomous action creates material risk of River or owner ruin. No leverage for unproven experimentation; guarantees and debt remain explicit owner-level decisions.
- **Expected loss budget.** Evaluate exploration as cohorts; a correctly failed probe is not a reason for pathological conservatism.
- **Evidence unlocks capital.** Observe → Probe → Validate → Incubate → Operate → Scale → Acquire, each stage with stronger evidence and controls. Scaling is itself an experiment: tranche, observe marginal return, continue only if economics persist.
- **Marginal allocation.** The question is the best use of the next marginal dollar or unit of capacity, not whether an engine is profitable.
- **Capital demands** carry resource, amount, purpose, evidence, unknowns, expected effect, maximum plausible loss, reversibility, duration, information value, salvage value, portfolio impact, stop rule, review condition; materiality-route so $3 decisions carry no bureaucracy.
- **Shadow costs** for compute, founder attention, support, legal capacity, security surface, trust, rights capacity, concentration, and complexity are planning costs, never booked expenses. Founder attention carries a deliberately high shadow value.
- **Internal capital market.** Represent grounded competing demands; use deterministic dominance (cheaper, better evidenced, more reversible, similarly valuable, better diversified, no compensating disadvantage) before any expensive reasoning. No fake precision: "A dominates B", "A probably dominates B but distribution economics are decision-critical unknown", or "current evidence does not justify ordering A and B". Scores must not launder narrative.
- **Shadow allocation** precedes material capital authority: Foundry says where it would allocate, reality compares, competence is evaluated by category, authority is granted accordingly.

## Cells, diversification, and resilience

A Cell is a bounded economic ecosystem where legitimate sharing of assets, distribution, customer understanding, capabilities, data, and knowledge creates advantage while maintaining an explicit blast radius. Cells emerge from observed economic locality (customer, distribution, data, capability, trust overlap), never from industry labels, and they pay rent: they form, split, merge, reproduce, and die on evidence. Cells are not legal entities; economic, brand, legal, and technical topology stay separate.

Concentrate locally, diversify globally. Not every asset is fungible: cash and compute largely are; trust, consent, purpose-limited rights, brand, and authority are not. Track material dependencies at account granularity (one Stripe account carrying most flow is a common failure domain), keep several risk graphs rather than one score, evaluate the diversification delta of each opportunity, and hunt anti-correlation when exposure concentrates. Resilience spend follows materiality. The immune system behaves rather than warns: circuit breakers scoped to engine, cell, capability, provider, or portfolio; degrade transparently, research substitutes, never fabricate.

## Scale economics

Hundreds or thousands of engines require that mature engines be cheap and quiet. The River sleeps by default and wakes intelligence on anomaly, customer problem, threshold, freshness issue, policy expiry, experiment conclusion, or external event. Institutional intensity follows materiality (Hot, Warm, Cold, Archived as a concept, not necessarily labels). **Marginal carry cost** is a fundamental River metric; the economic floor is the **Stewardship Floor** (the smallest thing Foundry can responsibly maintain), not the Production Floor. Creation throughput must not exceed demonstrated stewardship capacity; enter Consolidation Mode when quality debt, carry cost, overhead, founder interruptions, or risk opacity rise. Institutional overhead relative to durable contribution should fall over time; deliberation must pay rent. Centralize only what becomes more valuable by being common; localize everything whose context, risk, rights, or economics are damaged by aggregation. Knowledge and capability earn blast radius; keep novel or conflicting lessons local until corroborated.

## Quality is constitutional

Every durable public River asset must have a credible independent reason someone intentionally uses it. Search traffic cannot be the only reason an asset exists. Quality dimensions: promise, outcome, data, decision, experience, operational, support, economic, trust. The **Promise Ledger**: material customer-facing claims trace to evidence and compile into obligations, responsibilities, controls, and monitoring; marketing does not live in a separate epistemic universe. Every serious engine answers a **Quality Contract** (what is promised, what must always be true, tolerable and intolerable errors, proof of delivery, disclosed limits, remedy, degraded-mode and shutdown conditions). Error asymmetry is engine-specific; metrics derive from the ValueEvent, never from engagement; floors come before optimization (Goodhart resistance). Quality debt gates scaling. Degrade honestly. Shared craft (clear writing, honest claims, mobile, speed, easy cancellation, accessibility, no dark patterns, no fake urgency) with distinct public brands.

Four tests: **Trust** (would we operate this if the customer could see exactly how it works?), **Craft** (would an intelligent stranger call it competent and intentional, or automated clutter?), **Silence** (could a mature engine deliver for 30 days without a frontier model?), **Stewardship** (can Foundry keep it useful without lying, rotting, bothering the founder, or disproportionate risk? If not, do not retain it, even if the probe made money).

Prefer legal boringness: B2B operational tools, clear-rights commercial data, business intelligence, directories, legitimate referral, digital goods, utilities, licensing. Caution around consumer finance, medical or legal advice, sensitive PII, children, employment decisions, regulated finance, aggressive automated contact, high-risk claims. Use qualified professional input when consequence requires it.

## Economic truth

Books and managerial economics differ; maintain both. Engine-level contribution: revenue − refunds − payment fees − marketplace commissions − AI/compute − API/data − attributable hosting − paid acquisition − variable fulfillment − support − chargebacks − commissions. Beyond contribution, track founder intervention, maintenance burden, complexity, and proof debt. **Flow**, not MRR, is the general concept: money moves because value was created and captured, attributable to engine, primitive, counterparty, settlement, costs, reversals, period, and evidence. Classify durability (ephemeral, short-lived, renewable, evergreen, structural) and ask how fast flow would decay if Foundry did nothing. River health depends on **regeneration** (new durable flow versus durable flow lost), regeneration efficiency, and regeneration reliability. Sale is not failure; sale readiness is operational clarity. Buy and build compete; acquisition targets are reconstructed into verified, seller-asserted, inferred, and unknown.

Portfolio regimes (Conserve, Steady, Explore, Exploit, Consolidate, Recover, Preserve) are conceptual; the principle is that the same $500 experiment is rational with strong reserves and irresponsible in a liquidity shock. In crisis: protect obligations, preserve liquidity, stop speculative commitments, maintain customer value, investigate, avoid panic, resume when healthy. In windfall: verify settlement, check refund and fraud exposure, understand cause, protect customers, assess durability, allocate deliberately.

## Fable

Fable competes for capital like everything else. A major capability improvement answers: what repeated friction justifies it, how many engines are affected, what the workaround costs, whether a cheaper alternative suffices, how it will be benchmarked, and how later value will be evaluated. Distinguish maintenance, capability extraction, and speculative institutional R&D; speculative platform work is budget-bounded. Fable's economic purpose is to lower venture production cost and time, validation cost, carry cost, stewardship cost, failure cost, recovery cost, and founder intervention while preserving quality, evidence fidelity, safety, adaptability, and authority correctness. The recursion is governed: repeated friction → capability thesis → benchmark → smallest implementation → challenge → compare → canary → outcome → cutover → delete obsolete path → broader rollout only when evidence warrants. Fast phenotype, slow genome.

## Founder experience

The cockpit answers, in order: Are things okay? What materially changed? What genuinely needs me? What meaningful work is underway? What is the River learning? Activity is epistemically typed (Observed, Concluded, Planned, Authorized, Attempted, Verified, Learned), never "Growth Agent working…". Interrupt only when authority genuinely requires the owner, consequence is material, and policy cannot resolve it; every interruption states why you, why now, what happens if delayed, the evidence, and the choices. Recurring interruptions reveal policy gaps: propose a bounded standing policy, the founder approves it explicitly. The interface gets simpler as the institution gets more complex; at 5,000 engines show River state, material change, exceptions, capital, regeneration, systemic risk, and opportunities, with ASK for arbitrary drilldown. Mobile first. No single deceptive River Score: health stays multidimensional (yield, regeneration, durability, autonomy, diversity, complexity, recoverability, liquidity, ownership, optionality, trust, quality, promise integrity, stewardship capacity, learning rate, founder dependence).

Owner absence matures from seven days (operations continue) to thirty (customer quality intact) to ninety (maintenance and regeneration continue) to extended (the portfolio adapts to external change). Owner replaceability: a successor could understand what exists, what earns, what costs, contracts, authority, risks, assets, shutdown paths, and saleability.

## Owner decisions recorded (2026-09-07)

- **Internal identity.** Foundry is the implicit operating institution. A founder never configures Foundry as another agent or company before receiving value. Platform-level effects resolve to an owner-held institution scope, never to a tenant that happens to share the name.
- **External identity.** Foundry is not automatically the public brand of River engines. Public identity stays local to the engine, product, cell, or company so experimental failure does not create portfolio-wide reputational coupling.
- **Proof 1.** The first encounter with economic reality is a $29 one-time Massachusetts commercial millwork bid brief, bounded exactly as recorded in `river/proof-1/`. Its outcome, not this document, writes the next specification.

## North star and master doctrine

Qualitatively: the River creates durable legitimate economic value faster than existing value decays, while requiring progressively less owner intervention and remaining capable of surviving the failure of any individual engine, provider, platform, channel, or venture thesis. The formula River Health = Regeneration × Durability × Diversity × Autonomy × Adaptability, constrained by capital, complexity, stewardship cost, legal risk, correlated dependency, founder attention, and quality/trust floors, is conceptual and is not to be implemented as a number unless evidence later proves a quantitative model useful. The moat is portfolio replenishment capability: regeneration rate relative to decay at acceptable stewardship cost.

Foundry deepens commitment only as independent reality justifies it. Signals earn investigation; evidence earns experiments; customer value earns engines; repeated outcomes earn capital; competent operation earns authority; repeated reuse earns shared capability; economic locality earns cells; material success earns hardening; durable low-touch flow earns quiet harvest; contradiction earns retreat; death earns salvage and learning; changed reality earns resurrection; and excess capital belongs to the owner when no superior internal use exists.
