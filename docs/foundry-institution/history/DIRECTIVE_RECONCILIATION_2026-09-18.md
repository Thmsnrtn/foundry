# Reconciliation of the master directive — 18 September 2026

The owner's master directive (constitution, economic intelligence and
development) was reconciled against repository truth, deployed truth and the
work of the River of Nickels tranches shipped the same day. This is the
record of where the institution already stood ahead of it, what it named
that was genuinely missing, what was redundant, premature or wrong, and what
was done. Unknowns are left unknown.

## Truth, in its kinds

- **Repository truth:** branch `claude/foundry-v3-north-star-finish-vxq2ea`,
  migrations 001–322, the full chain green at every deploy this day.
- **Deployed truth:** `foundry-intel` on Fly reports the commit it runs;
  the V3 owner surface, the charter, the six public eyes, the forge and the
  first recipe of the hands are live. The public site republishes itself
  hourly from rows.
- **Externally proven:** the Workshop's site is read back from its address;
  the first experiment wrote to twenty-one businesses under the owner's
  approval and settled with no sale; the second was listed by the owner's
  own hand on a venue. **No purchase has ever been observed.** That is the
  state of economic proof and nothing above changes it.
- **Runtime-proven only:** the forge's deliberation, the brief made of rows,
  the venue path and the charter's admission of a test are proven on rows in
  tests, with the models stubbed. Whether the daily pass designs a real
  candidate in production is unknown until the first pass after a promotion.
- **Unknown:** whether any real seed has been promoted since the eyes were
  opened; the state of the second experiment's listing; whether the owner
  will sign a charter.

## Where Foundry was already ahead of the directive

- Bounds are already bounds, not weights: the legal and destructive rungs
  are never absorbable; owner exclusions outrank scores; the kill switch
  halts everything. (§5, §67)
- Sealed predictions, settlement rules, validity kept apart from verdict, and
  a return leg from the world already exist (migrations 238, 278). (§38, §51)
- The consequential-effect inventory, the outbound gateway, the erasure
  classification of every table, and the reachability gate already answer
  §13–§14's containment questions for the institution's own effects.
- Cognition follows surprise: `shouldThink`, the purpose ledger and the
  frontier warrant table already make stable reasoning cheap. (§37)
- The owner surface already follows §73–§74 (Letter, Decisions, Controls,
  Story) and the V3 North Star was finished this day.

## What the directive named that was genuinely missing, and what was done

- **Standing authority the owner signs once** (§6, §10, §84): the charter.
  Built, and the constitution amended explicitly rather than by implication.
- **Independent ways of knowing** (§27–§29): six public eyes, asked the
  question each can answer; the promotion bar now met on rows.
- **A forge that composes under discipline** (§31–§35): five lenses recorded
  before composition, one composer, one adversary, a seal that is neither.
- **A recipe the hands can execute with a gate that refuses** (§7, §17): the
  brief made of rows; the venue that needs no list of people.
- **The world following the record** (§10): the public site republished
  from rows on the hour.

## Redundant, premature, or wrong

- **Redundant:** a new pipeline for the flywheel (§80); the existing
  discovery → seed → candidate → experiment → asset → settlement chain is
  it. A safety plane or ownership shield as a subsystem (§9, §12): the
  health, absence, exposure and legal-surface machinery already carry most
  of it; what is missing is coverage, not a plane.
- **Premature:** the economic observatory (§39–§58), the markets laboratory
  and capital governor (§59–§65), acquisition (§23), deep economic memory
  (§42). Each waits on a real economic responsibility, as the directive
  itself says (§58). Nothing was built for them.
- **Wrong as stated:** "the owner should not need to remember any of this"
  (§9) cannot be true for the two things only he holds — the sending of
  money and the signature — and the institution should say so rather than
  pretend. Also, one promoted candidate's cheapest test was assumed absent
  in the first reading of §86 and was not: the reality-only rule already
  supplies it. The severed link was the words the hands can build from,
  and that is what was connected.

## The narrowest real constraint now

Between a sealed design and a sale stand: the owner's signature on a
charter (owner), the postal line he typed (owner), a payment provider and
sending already configured in production (present), and reach beyond the
Workshop's own visitors (channels, next). Reality's part — whether anybody
pays — is not the institution's to supply.
