## The claim this directive makes that reality contradicts

The directive assumes the communications membrane must largely be built. It
mostly exists. Migration 131 already established provider-neutral inbound
customer communication, and its own header states the invariant this directive
asks for in Part CXXXV, in almost the same words: *"a customer message is
evidence that someone outside the company said something. It is not a
responsibility, not authority, not consent."* Migration 132 already refused to
create a proposal table, routing founder-authored replies through
`signal_events` and plans through `outbound_actions`, preserving *message ≠
proposal ≠ consent ≠ plan ≠ execution ≠ receipt ≠ business outcome*.
`prompt-shield.ts` already sanitises untrusted content before it reaches a
model.

So the membrane is not a new institution. **It is a missing path**: mail
arriving at `apexmicro.ai` is forwarded to a Gmail account and never reaches
Foundry at all.

## Classification

**Already embodied.** Evidence/interpretation/judgment separation; obligations
surviving stop and pause; governed effects with receipts, verification and
rollback; ambiguous-effect reconciliation before retry; Workshop-wide
suppression; scoped continuation; sealed prospective deliberation; exchange
context on commercial observations; multidimensional stop conditions;
fulfilment cap; true-probe-cost doctrine; distribution-cleanliness doctrine;
reputation-as-shared-asset doctrine; owner-only pause; erasure across every
table; the fail-closed outbound chain; inbound message evidence semantics;
reply proposals and plans; prompt sanitisation; responsibility/agent
separation; capability ≠ authority.

**Doctrinal clarification, not code.** Ethical admissibility as a gate rather
than a weight; positive-sum preference; the hard-constraint ordering; value
creation ≠ perception ≠ capture ≠ payment capability ≠ recurrence ≠ durability;
behaviour outranks praise; do-nothing as a baseline; probe dominance; value of
information; commitment as contextual rather than a universal ladder; scale as
a separate hypothesis; sufficiency; cash option value; owner distribution as a
success state; selectivity rising as creation gets cheaper.

**Missing operational capability — and the reason for this tranche.** Mail
reaching `apexmicro.ai` cannot reach Foundry. Everything downstream of that —
inbound obligations, opt-outs by reply, continuation by reply, customer
evidence, owner inbox — is unreachable in production, however well modelled.

**Missing invariant.** Nothing structurally prevents inbound message content
from being treated as instruction at the point where a model reads it for the
Workshop; `prompt-shield` is available but not compulsory on this path, and
this path does not yet exist.

**Missing owner surface.** No inbox. The owner cannot see Apex Micro
correspondence from Foundry at all.

**Not yet earned, deliberately deferred.** The Experiment Atlas as a schema;
enum-heavy classification ontologies; a dependency/blast-radius graph (one
Workshop, one zone, one payment provider — the graph would have one shape and
teach nothing); portfolio correlation modelling (one experiment, zero
customers); treasury/distribution machinery (zero revenue); acquisition,
two-sided and exit experiment species (no territory earns them); email
crystallisation into templates (no recurring traffic exists to crystallise);
autonomy escalation beyond draft (no proven response quality); communication
economics attribution (no communications volume).

**The honest constraint on all of it: nobody has been contacted.** Zero
contacts, zero outcome events, zero customers. An institution cannot learn
experiment-design Experience Capital, communication patterns, evidence decay or
portfolio correlation from an empty world. Building those now would be
modelling a reality that has not happened — the exact failure the directive
warns against in Part CXVII.

## What this tranche builds

The one thing whose absence blocks everything else, at its smallest honest
size: **a governed path from the Apex Micro mailbox into the institution**,
extending the evidence spine that already exists rather than creating a second
one, with the security invariant made structural rather than available.

Everything else in this directive that is not already embodied is doctrine, or
waits on a first real encounter with a person.
