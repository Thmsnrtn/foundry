# Adversarial review of the First Economic Closure tranche (2026-09-06)

Six adversarial readers over `168ee901..cee62e98` produced 34 findings; the nine most severe were each handed to a refuter told to default to refuted, and all nine were reproduced. Every confirmed finding and every unverified one was then either fixed with a test or recorded here with the reason it was not. Nothing was left silent.

## Confirmed, and what was done

### [high] A durable floor is bypassed whenever the model names the floor's class but omits (or nulls) the floor's structural fact
`src/services/venture/legal-pass.ts:349`

**Scenario.** Candidate headline 'A page that decides whether a named tenant may rent, for landlords'. Model reply: surfaces=[{class:'regulation', standing:'recognised', severity:'minor', grounds:'decides whether a named tenant may rent'}], facts=[] (or regulated_decision/decision_about_named_person with present:null). **Done.** fixed: a surface the model names in a floor class carries the floor unless the record established the fact absent (legal-pass.ts); test added.
