# Competitor: Runway
Category: Founder OS / CEO cockpit
URL: https://runway.com

## What They Do
Runway is a modern FP&A (Financial Planning & Analysis) platform that replaces spreadsheets with collaborative, real-time financial modeling, forecasting, and reporting. It gives founders and finance teams a single environment to build revenue models, plan headcount, run scenario analyses, and produce board-ready reports — all connected to live data from 750+ integrations.

## Target User
- Startup founders preparing for fundraising (seed through Series C)
- First finance hires and heads of finance at 50-500 person companies
- FP&A directors and CFOs at high-growth and enterprise companies (500-1,500+ employees)
- Cross-functional teams (sales, marketing, ops) collaborating on budgets

## Key Features
- Driver-based financial modeling with human-readable formulas
- Scenario planning with sandbox environments (base/upside/downside)
- Headcount planning with instant budget impact visibility
- Revenue forecasting tied to CRM pipeline data
- Collaborative budgeting with approval workflows
- Automated variance analysis (AI-powered)
- Interactive dashboards and board-ready reporting
- 750+ integrations (QuickBooks, Stripe, Salesforce, Gusto, Snowflake, NetSuite, etc.)
- Multi-entity consolidation and dimensional segmentation

## Pricing
Custom pricing — not publicly listed. Runway emphasizes "transparent pricing that isn't user-based" but requires a scoping conversation. Estimated $1,000-3,000+/mo based on company size and data complexity. Backed by $33.5M in funding (a16z, Initialized Capital).

## Strengths (vs Foundry)
- Deep, purpose-built financial modeling engine — years of refinement on FP&A workflows
- 750+ native integrations across accounting, HRIS, CRM, and data warehouses
- Strong brand recognition in finance/startup ecosystem (4.8/5 on G2)
- Spreadsheet-familiar UX lowers adoption barrier for finance teams
- Enterprise-grade: multi-entity, multi-region, dimensional segmentation
- Implementation support with ~6-week average deployment

## Weaknesses (vs Foundry)
- Finance-only scope — no visibility into product, engineering, customer success, or marketing operations
- Passive tool: requires humans to build models, interpret data, and take action
- No autonomous agents — cannot proactively surface issues or execute decisions
- Single-company focus — no multi-company portfolio view for founders running multiple ventures
- No AI-driven recommendations or automated playbook execution
- Requires dedicated finance expertise to get full value from the platform

## Key Insight
Runway solves the financial visibility problem exceptionally well but stops at the dashboard. Foundry's opportunity is to subsume the financial intelligence layer (possibly integrating with tools like Runway) while adding autonomous action — the leap from "here's what your burn looks like" to "I've already renegotiated that vendor contract and shifted the hiring plan." Multi-company orchestration is Foundry's structural moat that Runway has no path toward.
