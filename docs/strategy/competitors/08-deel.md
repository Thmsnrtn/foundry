# Competitor: Deel
Category: Startup OS / Back-Office Tools
URL: https://www.deel.com

## What They Do
Deel is an all-in-one global HR and workforce platform that handles hiring, payroll, compliance, IT device management, and employee engagement across 150+ countries through its own legal entities. Originally an Employer of Record (EOR) service, Deel has expanded into a comprehensive back-office platform with HRIS, contractor management, benefits administration, immigration support, and AI-powered HR automation. They aim to be the single system of record for everything workforce-related.

## Target User
Distributed and remote-first companies hiring internationally, from 10-person startups to enterprises with thousands of global employees. Finance teams managing multi-country payroll, HR leaders handling compliance across jurisdictions, and founders scaling without wanting to set up local entities.

## Key Features
- Employer of Record (EOR) in 150+ countries via owned entities
- Global payroll processing consolidated into a single system
- Contractor management with compliant contracts and payments
- Free HRIS tier (up to 200 people): employee records, PTO, org charts
- Deel Engage: performance reviews, feedback cycles, learning tools
- Deel IT: device provisioning, software management, IT compliance
- Deel Benefits Admin: local benefits enrollment and tracking
- Deel Immigration: work visa and permit processing
- AI "HR personas" for payroll validation, leave approvals, compliance audits
- Workflow automation and customizable onboarding checklists
- Background checks, equity management, and expense tracking
- Native integrations with accounting, HRIS, and collaboration tools

## Pricing
- **HRIS**: Free for up to 200 people; paid tiers from $5/employee/month
- **Contractor management**: $49/contractor/month
- **Global Payroll**: From $29/employee/month
- **Employer of Record**: From $599/employee/month
- **Deel Engage**: $20/employee/month
- **Deel IT**: $99/month base
- Add-ons: Background checks ($30-$200), work permits ($1,500-$5,000), premium support (custom)
- Total cost for complex hires can be 20-30% above advertised base prices

## Strengths (vs Foundry)
- Deepest global compliance moat: 150+ countries with owned legal entities
- Massive product surface area covering HR, payroll, IT, and compliance in one platform
- Free HRIS tier creates a low-friction entry point
- Regulatory expertise and local legal knowledge that is extremely hard to replicate
- Strong network effects from processing payroll data across thousands of companies
- AI-powered compliance automation is a genuine differentiator in a regulated domain

## Weaknesses (vs Foundry)
- Exclusively workforce-focused: no coverage of metrics, revenue, product, or strategy
- No autonomous agents that proactively identify risks or opportunities across the business
- Expensive at scale: $599/employee/month EOR pricing with hidden add-on costs
- Platform complexity overwhelms small teams that do not need global compliance
- No portfolio-level view for founders running multiple companies
- Limited performance management and analytics depth compared to dedicated tools
- Inconsistent support quality, especially in APAC time zones (8-12 hour delays)
- Does not address financial operations, fundraising, or strategic decision-making
- Ongoing regulatory scrutiny around contractor classification practices

## Key Insight
Deel demonstrates that compliance and regulatory complexity create defensible moats -- their 150+ country entity network is nearly impossible to replicate. But their expansion from EOR into "all-in-one HR OS" shows the limits of a workforce-only lens. Foundry's opportunity is to be the layer above tools like Deel: an autonomous control plane that ingests signals from Deel (and Mercury, and Stripe, and others) to make cross-functional decisions that no single back-office tool can see. Deel manages your people; Foundry manages your company.
