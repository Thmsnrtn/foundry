# Competitor: Totem VC
Category: Holding-company / portfolio operator tool
URL: https://www.totemvc.com/

## What They Do
Totem is an end-to-end AI fund operating system that covers the full investment lifecycle — from deal flow and portfolio management to data collection and LP reporting. Born out of a VC fund, it has spent 8+ years building infrastructure for fund operations, supporting billions in AUM across hundreds of funds and 800+ portfolio companies. It also offers back-office services (legal, accounting, tax filings, audits) through its Totem One product.

## Target User
VC fund managers and multi-fund operators who need a single system of record across deal sourcing, portfolio tracking, cap table management, data collection, and LP reporting. Especially suited for firms running multiple funds simultaneously.

## Key Features
- Deal flow management with pipeline tracking and investment committee workflows
- Portfolio management with cap table integration, ownership records, and co-investor tracking
- Automated data collection via structured surveys sent to portfolio companies
- AI-powered meeting transcription with summarized decisions and action items
- LP reporting with standardized templates and automated data population
- Custom presentation and report generation using all fund and company data
- Automatic transaction import from back-office systems
- Fund performance tracking with tear sheets, valuations, and key metrics
- Totem One: bundled fund administration services (legal, accounting, tax, audit)
- Board meeting prep, investment committee review, and fundraising support tools

## Pricing
Custom pricing — not publicly listed. Enterprise-oriented; requires sales consultation. Positioned as premium given the breadth of fund operations covered and the bundled back-office services.

## Strengths (vs Foundry)
- Full lifecycle coverage — handles everything from deal sourcing through fund administration, not just monitoring
- Deep fund operations expertise — 8 years of domain knowledge built by VCs for VCs
- Back-office services (Totem One) create high switching costs and stickiness
- Structured data collection from portfolio companies is battle-tested at scale (800+ companies)
- AI-native positioning with meeting transcription, automated reporting, and data extraction

## Weaknesses (vs Foundry)
- Fund-manager perspective, not operator perspective — designed for investors overseeing portfolio companies, not founders/operators running them day-to-day
- No autonomous execution — AI assists with reporting and transcription but does not independently take operational actions across companies
- No cross-company operational control plane — cannot deploy code, manage billing, handle support tickets, or execute business logic across portfolio companies
- Single-tenant mental model — each fund is a silo; not designed for a single operator who is the CEO/founder of multiple businesses simultaneously
- AI is augmentation, not autonomy — Totem uses AI to summarize and generate reports, but agents do not monitor, decide, and act independently

## Key Insight
Totem represents the most operationally ambitious competitor in this category — it goes beyond monitoring into fund administration and lifecycle management. But its ambition is bounded by the VC fund paradigm: the system helps fund managers track and report on companies they have invested in, not companies they operate. Foundry's differentiation is the shift from "portfolio oversight" to "portfolio control" — where AI agents do not just report that revenue dropped but autonomously investigate, diagnose, and fix the issue across any company in the portfolio.
