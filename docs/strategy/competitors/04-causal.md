# Competitor: Causal (now part of Lucanet)
Category: Founder OS / CEO cockpit
URL: https://www.causal.app

## What They Do
Causal is a cloud-based financial modeling platform that lets founders and finance teams build dynamic models using plain-English variables instead of spreadsheet formulas. It emphasizes scenario planning, visual dashboards, and investor-ready reporting. In late 2024, Causal was acquired by Lucanet (a German CPM company), repositioning it as part of a broader "Extended Planning & Analysis" (xP&A) enterprise suite.

## Target User
- Startup founders building investor-ready financial models (10-25 person companies)
- SaaS companies modeling recurring revenue, churn, and unit economics
- Finance teams managing forecasting and board reporting (up to 1,000 person companies)
- Operations teams tracking departmental budgets

## Key Features
- Driver-based financial modeling with plain-English formulas
- Scenario planning (base, upside, downside) without duplicating models
- Visual dashboards and charts for board presentations and investor updates
- Pre-built templates for SaaS forecasting, cash flow, and startup reporting
- Data integrations with QuickBooks, Xero, Gusto, BambooHR, HubSpot
- Collaborative browser-based editing with version comparison
- Headcount planning and sales forecasting modules
- Sensitivity analysis and uncertainty modeling

## Pricing
- **Modelling plan**: $250/month — 2 members included (additional at $49/mo each), unlimited models with 1M cell limit, 1 standard data integration, scenario planning, headcount planning, sales forecasting
- **Business plan**: Custom pricing — for 50-1,000 person companies, expanded integrations and controls
- **Enterprise (Lucanet xP&A)**: Custom pricing — full CPM suite

## Strengths (vs Foundry)
- Approachable pricing entry point ($250/mo) for early-stage founders
- Plain-English formula syntax genuinely lowers the modeling barrier
- Strong scenario planning UX — best-in-class for "what if" fundraising prep
- Pre-built SaaS templates get founders to useful models quickly
- Visual output quality is high — investor-ready without design effort

## Weaknesses (vs Foundry)
- Acquired by Lucanet — product direction now driven by enterprise CPM priorities, not startup needs
- Post-acquisition degradation: users report worse support, billing issues, and slower feature development
- Finance-only scope — no operational, product, or customer success visibility
- Completely passive — requires manual model building and interpretation
- No autonomous capabilities — cannot detect problems or take action on behalf of founders
- No multi-company portfolio management
- Limited integrations compared to competitors (no Stripe, Snowflake, or Salesforce native)
- 1M cell limit on base plan constrains complex modeling

## Key Insight
Causal's acquisition by Lucanet is a cautionary tale and an opportunity: startup-focused tools get absorbed into enterprise stacks and lose their founder-centric identity. Foundry should position itself as permanently aligned with multi-company founders — not a tool that will pivot to enterprise finance departments. The gap Causal leaves behind (affordable, founder-first financial intelligence) is exactly where Foundry's autonomous agents can step in with zero-effort financial monitoring that doesn't require building models at all.
