# Lens 27 — Growth Strategist Audit

**Auditor perspective:** Growth strategist
**Scope:** Activation, retention, expansion loops, referral mechanisms, viral loops, onboarding funnel optimization, Day 1 to Day 7 retention strategy
**Date:** 2026-04-16

---

## Executive Summary

Foundry has a **well-designed activation path** (signup -> GitHub -> audit -> tour -> dashboard) and a **strong autonomous retention mechanism** (weekly digests, daily insights, behavioral triggers). However, there is **no referral mechanism, no viral loop, no expansion incentive beyond tier upgrades, and no systematic Day 1 to Day 7 retention strategy**. The share link feature (investor/advisor read-only view) is the closest thing to a viral mechanism, but it targets investors, not potential new founders. The founding cohort concept was retired with no replacement acquisition loop. Growth is entirely dependent on top-of-funnel marketing with no product-led growth mechanics.

**P1 findings:** 3
**P2 findings:** 4

## Finding 01 — No referral or invite mechanism

**Severity: P1**
**Files:** Entire codebase (absence)

There is no "invite a founder" feature, no referral code system, no affiliate program, and no "share Foundry" prompt. The share link (`/share/:token`) creates a read-only view for investors -- it does not lead to signup. There is no link from the share page back to a Foundry signup flow.

For a $79-$399/mo B2B product targeting SaaS founders, the most effective growth channel is peer recommendation. Founders trust other founders. The product has no mechanism to capture or amplify this.

**Impact:** Zero product-led acquisition. Every new founder must be acquired through marketing or direct sales.

**Remediation:**
1. Add a "Recommend Foundry" feature that generates a unique referral link per founder.
2. Offer a meaningful incentive (e.g., one month free for both referrer and referee, or a credit toward the next tier).
3. Add a CTA to the share page: "Want this for your own product? Start free."
4. Surface the referral prompt at high-satisfaction moments (e.g., after first READY verdict, after score improves 2+ points).

## Finding 02 — No Day 1 to Day 7 retention strategy

**Severity: P1**
**Files:** `src/services/triggers/behavioral.ts`, `src/services/ux/tour.ts`

The activation flow (onboarding -> first audit -> tour) is designed for Day 1. After that, the retention strategy relies on:
- **Behavioral trigger emails** that fire at 24h if GitHub is not connected and at 7d if no action is taken after audit. These are rescue mechanisms for stalled users, not proactive retention.
- **Weekly digest** delivered on Monday mornings. But this is a 7-day gap from signup.
- **Daily insights** generated by a cron job, but these appear only when the founder visits the dashboard -- there is no push notification or email for daily insights.

There is no proactive engagement between Day 2 and Day 7:
- No "Day 2: Complete your Product DNA" email
- No "Day 3: Your first daily insight is ready" notification
- No "Day 5: Here is what your agents found this week" preview
- No drip sequence educating founders on features they have not used

**Impact:** Founders who complete onboarding on Day 1 may not return until the first digest on Day 7 (or 14, depending on timing). This creates a critical retention gap.

**Remediation:**
1. Design a 7-day onboarding drip sequence (email or in-app notifications):
   - Day 1: Welcome + first audit complete
   - Day 2: Prompt to complete Product DNA (explain why it matters)
   - Day 3: First daily insight notification (email if they have not logged in)
   - Day 5: Agent system overview + first competitive scan preview
   - Day 7: First digest + milestone summary
2. Use the existing notification and trigger infrastructure to implement this.

## Finding 03 — No expansion loop beyond tier upgrades

**Severity: P1**
**Files:** `src/services/billing/stripe.ts`, `src/middleware/tier-gate.ts`

Expansion revenue comes only from tier upgrades (Solo $79 -> Growth $199 -> Investor-Ready $399). There is no:
- **Seat-based pricing** (team members cannot be invited as paid seats)
- **Usage-based pricing** (AI call volume, number of audits, number of integrations)
- **Add-on pricing** (premium features, additional products beyond tier limit)
- **Annual pricing** with prepayment discount

The team invitation system (`routes/dashboard/team.ts`) exists but team members appear to be free additions with no billing impact. The multi-product capability is tier-gated (Investor-Ready only) but does not charge per additional product.

**Impact:** Revenue per founder is capped at $399/mo regardless of value delivered. Large-scale users subsidize the platform without proportional contribution.

**Remediation:**
1. Introduce annual pricing (e.g., 2 months free) to improve retention and cash flow.
2. Consider per-product pricing for Investor-Ready tier (base + $X per additional product).
3. Evaluate usage-based AI credit system to align cost with value.

## Finding 04 — Share link has no acquisition funnel

**Severity: P2**
**Files:** `src/routes/share/index.ts`

The investor share page displays a read-only Signal score, metrics, and recent decisions. It is a polished page that showcases Foundry's value. However:
- There is no "Powered by Foundry" branding with a link to the landing page.
- There is no CTA for the viewer (investor/advisor) to recommend Foundry to other portfolio companies.
- The page is a dead end -- it shows data but leads nowhere.

This is a missed organic acquisition channel. Investors who see impressive Signal dashboards may want to recommend Foundry to other portfolio founders.

**Impact:** Every share page view is a wasted acquisition opportunity.

**Remediation:**
1. Add a subtle "Powered by Foundry" footer with a link to the landing page.
2. Add a CTA: "Want this for your portfolio companies? Learn more."
3. Track share page views as a top-of-funnel metric.

## Finding 05 — Milestone celebrations are not shareable

**Severity: P2**
**Files:** `src/services/ux/milestones.ts`

Milestones (first audit complete, READY verdict, score above 7, etc.) generate in-app notifications and Founding Story artifacts. These are natural high-satisfaction moments. However:
- There is no "Share this milestone" button (e.g., to Twitter/LinkedIn).
- The milestone content is not formatted for social sharing (no Open Graph image, no shareable URL).
- There is no prompt to write a testimonial at milestone moments.

**Impact:** High-satisfaction moments are contained within the product and do not generate organic social proof.

**Remediation:**
1. Add a "Share this achievement" button on milestone toasts that generates a pre-formatted social post.
2. Create a public milestone page with Open Graph tags for each significant milestone.
3. Prompt founders for a brief testimonial at the "READY verdict" milestone.

## Finding 06 — No re-engagement mechanism for churned or paused founders

**Severity: P2**
**Files:** `src/services/billing/stripe.ts`

The `cancelSubscription` function cancels the Stripe subscription and the webhook handler sets `tier = NULL`. After that, there is no:
- Win-back email sequence
- "Here is what you missed" periodic email showing how the product evolved
- Re-activation offer
- Data retention notice (your data is still here, come back anytime)

Similarly, `pauseSubscription` voids collection but there is no re-engagement sequence for paused subscriptions.

**Impact:** Churned founders are lost forever. No attempt is made to recover them.

**Remediation:**
1. On cancellation, trigger a 3-email win-back sequence (Day 1: "We're sorry to see you go", Day 14: "Your product data is still here", Day 30: "Here's what's new in Foundry").
2. On pause, send a monthly "Your product update" email showing what the SCP agents would have found.
3. Track cancellation reasons (add a cancellation survey before processing).

## Finding 07 — Onboarding has two paths but no path optimization

**Severity: P2**
**Files:** `src/routes/dashboard/onboarding.ts`

There are two onboarding paths: GitHub-connected (code-based audit) and URL-based (no-code audit). The choice is presented at the start. However:
- There is no data on which path has higher activation rates.
- The no-code path appears less developed (fewer features, no audit engine integration visible).
- There is no way to switch paths after starting.
- There is no guidance on which path to choose based on the founder's product.

**Impact:** Founders may choose the wrong path and drop off. Without data, the team cannot optimize either path.

**Remediation:**
1. Track path selection and completion rates separately.
2. Add guidance: "If your product has a GitHub repository, choose the code path for a deeper analysis."
3. Allow switching paths mid-flow.
