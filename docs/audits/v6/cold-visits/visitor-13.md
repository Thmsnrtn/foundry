# Cold Visitor 13 — Indie Hacker with 2 Micro-SaaS Products

## First Impression (5 seconds)
"Connect your product. Get a company." — immediately resonant. I run 2 micro-SaaS products completely solo. I AM the team. "12 specialized AI agents" doing marketing, support, analytics, code quality — that's literally every hat I wear. The CTA "Give my product a team" speaks directly to me.

## 30-Second Scan
Every agent in the 12-agent grid maps to something I do manually today. Beacon (marketing) — I tweet and write blog posts at 11pm. Harbor (CS) — I answer support emails between coding sessions. Ledger (revenue) — I check Stripe once a week and hope for the best. Oracle (analytics) — I barely look at PostHog. The daily CEO briefing example is beautiful — one morning email with everything I need. The evolution engine means the more I use it, the better it gets.

## Category Identification
Score: 5/5 — "This is an AI team for solo SaaS founders. Crystal clear. I am the exact person who needs this."

## Value Prop
Score: 5/5 — "The value prop is 'stop being a solo founder wearing 12 hats.' I do all of these roles badly because there aren't enough hours. If even half these agents work, I'd get my evenings back. The daily briefing would replace my chaotic morning of checking 6 dashboards."

## Pricing

Investor-Ready ($399) says 5 companies but that's overkill and 5x the cost. For a micro-SaaS founder making $3K MRR across both products, $199/mo is 6.6% of revenue — meaningful. I wish there was a '$119 for 2 companies' tier. The pricing gap between Solo and Investor-Ready for multi-product is painful."

## Signup Readiness
Score: 4/5 — "I'd sign up for Solo at $79/mo to test with my primary product. The price is right, the promise is right, and the risk is low. My only hesitation is the lack of a free trial. $79 isn't much but indie hackers are frugal — even a 7-day trial would push me over the edge."

## First-Run Mental Model
