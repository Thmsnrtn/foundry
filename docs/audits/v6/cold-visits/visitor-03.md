# Cold Visitor 03 — Family Office Principal (10 companies under management)

## First Impression (5 seconds)
"Connect your product. Get a company." — I manage 10 companies, I don't need to "get" one. "Sovereign Company Protocol" sounds interesting from a governance perspective but I can't tell what it means. The hero talks about "your SaaS product" — I oversee a portfolio, not a single product.

## 30-Second Scan
Reads the 12-agent grid. These agents are operational — CTO, CMO, CS, CFO. This is positioned as a replacement for hiring, not as a portfolio management tool. The CEO briefing example shows one company's daily metrics. I manage 10 companies — I need a consolidated portfolio view, risk dashboards across entities, comparative performance. None of that is on this page.

## Category Identification
Score: 2/5 — "I think this is an AI-powered virtual team for individual SaaS products. It's not a portfolio management tool. The 'CFO' agent tracks revenue for one company, not across my family office. I can't tell if this is an operating system for founders or a monitoring tool for investors."

## Value Prop
Score: 2/5 — "The problem this solves is 'solo founder needs a team.' My problem is 'principal needs visibility and governance across 10 entities.' Different problem. The Investor-Ready tier mentions 'board packets' and 'funding readiness score' which are closer to my needs, but those are for founders approaching investors, not for the investor themselves."

## Pricing
Score: 2/5 — "The max tier supports 5 companies at $399/mo. I have 10. There's no enterprise or custom plan mentioned. No 'Contact Sales' option. Even if I could buy two $399 plans, that's $798/mo for what might not even solve my portfolio visibility problem. I can't predict my cost for 10 companies."

## Signup Readiness
Score: 1/5 — "Would not sign up. The product caps at 5 companies, I have 10. There's no enterprise tier, no 'Contact Us,' no way to engage at my scale. Even the problem it solves isn't quite my problem."

## First-Run Mental Model
Score: 2/5 — "If I did sign up, I imagine I'd set up 5 companies and get 5 separate daily briefings. But I want one consolidated view. The page doesn't show any portfolio-level intelligence. I'd be disappointed."

## Would They Identify as Target User?
No — "The footer says 'SaaS Founders.' I'm a principal managing investments. The VC partner might find the Investor-Ready tier interesting, but even that is framed for founders, not investors."

## Key Quote
"This is a founder tool, not a portfolio tool. Interesting concept but it doesn't speak my language or solve my problem at my scale."
