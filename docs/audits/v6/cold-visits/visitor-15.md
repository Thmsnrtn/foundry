# Cold Visitor 15 — Accelerator Program Manager

## First Impression (5 seconds)
"Connect your product. Get a company." — interesting premise. "12 specialized AI agents" — I manage a cohort of 20 startups per batch. Could this be a tool I recommend to my cohort companies? Or could I use it to monitor their progress? The page speaks to a single founder, not a program manager.

## 30-Second Scan
The agent roster covers exactly the functional areas where early-stage founders struggle: marketing, CS, finance, code quality. If my cohort companies each used Foundry, they'd get AI coaching in the areas where they're weakest. The CEO briefing would be a great way for founders to stay on top of operations. The Investor-Ready tier has "board packets" and "funding readiness score" — directly relevant for accelerator companies approaching demo day. But there's no "batch" or "program" view.

## Category Identification
Score: 4/5 — "AI virtual operations team for SaaS founders. Clear category. I can see how this fits into an accelerator's toolkit — like recommending Stripe Atlas for incorporation, you'd recommend Foundry for operations."

## Value Prop
Score: 3/5 — "The value prop for individual founders is strong. The value prop for me as a program manager is indirect: better-operated companies in my cohort. But I can't monitor their progress from my seat. I'd want a 'program manager dashboard' showing signal scores, risk states, and milestone progress across 20 companies. That doesn't exist here."

## Pricing
Score: 3/5 — "$79/company/month for Solo. For a 20-company cohort, that's $1,580/mo. Could the accelerator pay this as a cohort benefit? Maybe. But there's no batch pricing, no accelerator partnership page, no volume discount. I'd need to negotiate something custom, and there's no 'Contact Us' or 'Partnerships' link."

## Signup Readiness
Score: 2/5 — "I wouldn't sign up personally. I'd consider recommending it to individual founders in my cohort, but without a partnership model or batch pricing, it's hard to justify programmatically. If they had an accelerator partnership page, I'd reach out."

## First-Run Mental Model
Score: 2/5 — "If I signed up, I'd be treated as a founder setting up my own product. That's not my use case. I'd want to create accounts for 20 companies and see a dashboard of their collective health. No such pathway exists."

## Would They Identify as Target User?
No — "I'm a program manager, not a founder. But I'd recommend this to founders IF there were proof it works and a batch deal."

## Key Quote
"Great concept for my cohort founders, but without batch pricing or a program dashboard, I can't operationalize it for the accelerator. Put together a partnership page and we'll talk."
