# Cold Visitor 04 — Startup Studio Operator (builds 4 companies/year)

## First Impression (5 seconds)
"Connect your product. Get a company." — provocative. I build companies for a living so the metaphor resonates. "12 specialized AI agents" — interesting, this could accelerate early-stage operations. Sees "Give my product a team" CTA. Thinking: can I use this to bootstrap the ops layer for each new company I spin up?

## 30-Second Scan
The 12-agent team is compelling for studio operations. When I launch a new product, I don't have a CMO, CFO, or CS person — the agents could fill those gaps in the first 6 months. The evolution engine ("agents that learn") means each company starts fresh but gets smarter over time. The How It Works flow — connect GitHub, fill DNA, agents activate — is exactly the kind of fast setup a studio needs.

## Category Identification
Score: 4/5 — "This is an AI-powered virtual operating team for SaaS products. Like hiring a fractional everything-team that's powered by AI. The 'Sovereign Company Protocol' label is jargon I'd ignore, but the substance is clear: connect a product, get an AI team."

## Value Prop
Score: 4/5 — "Strong fit for studio operations. I build 4 companies/year and can't staff each one fully. If these agents actually work, they solve the 'too many companies, not enough people' problem. The daily CEO briefing per company would give me the oversight I need. My concern: can agents handle pre-product-market-fit chaos, or do they need a mature product?"

## Pricing
Score: 3/5 — "I'd need the Investor-Ready tier at $399/mo for up to 5 companies. At 4 companies/year this works, but what happens when I have 6+ active portfolio companies? No enterprise option. Also, $399/mo per studio seems reasonable, but I'd want to understand: when I spin down a company, can I remove it and add a new one? Lifecycle management isn't addressed."

## Signup Readiness
Score: 3/5 — "I'd seriously consider it. The $399/mo for 5 companies is within budget for a studio. But I'd want to test it on one company first before committing the full portfolio. A trial or a Solo plan test drive would get me in the door faster. No free trial mentioned."

## First-Run Mental Model
Score: 3/5 — "I'd expect to add a new company, connect its repo, define its DNA, and have agents start working. But I'm unclear on: how long until agents are useful? Days? Weeks? The page says agents 'start cautious' which implies slow ramp. For a studio, I need value in week 1, not month 3."

## Would They Identify as Target User?
Yes — "The product is for SaaS founders. I'm a SaaS founder, times four. The multi-company tier fits. I just need confidence it works for early-stage products, not just established ones."

## Key Quote
"If this actually works, it's the ops layer every studio needs. I'd test it on my next launch and see if the agents deliver anything useful in the first two weeks."
