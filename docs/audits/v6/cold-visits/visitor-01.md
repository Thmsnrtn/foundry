# Cold Visitor 01 — Solo SaaS Founder (1 product, no second yet)

## First Impression (5 seconds)
Sees "Give Your Product a Team" as the page title. Below, "Sovereign Company Protocol" in small caps. The headline reads "Connect your product. Get a company." Immediately intrigued but slightly confused — "get a company" is ambiguous. Sees a call-to-action "Give my product a team" and a link to pricing.

## 30-Second Scan
Scrolls down. Sees the vision statement: "a founder opens Foundry, connects their product, and gets a company... with a team of specialized AI agents." Now it clicks — this is AI agents that run your business. Sees the 12-agent grid (Atlas CTO, Beacon CMO, etc.). The "How It Works" section clarifies: connect GitHub, agents activate, read daily briefing, agents evolve. The CEO briefing example is compelling — MRR tracking, agent actions, decisions waiting for approval.

## Category Identification
Score: 3/5 — "I think this is an AI agent platform for SaaS operations... but 'Sovereign Company Protocol' and 'Get a company' made me think it was about incorporation or entity formation for the first few seconds. Once I read the subhead I got it, but the hero confused me."

## Value Prop
Score: 4/5 — "The value prop is clear once I scroll past the hero: 12 AI agents handle marketing, support, analytics, code quality, etc. while I sleep. The daily briefing example is the most convincing part — that's exactly what I want. But I wonder how much of this is real vs. vaporware. There's no social proof, no testimonials, no metrics from real users."

## Pricing
Score: 4/5 — "I'd click through to pricing. $79/mo for Solo with 1 company and 12 agents is clear and predictable. No per-seat gotchas. No usage-based surprises mentioned. The tiers make sense for my trajectory — I'd start Solo and maybe upgrade to Growth ($199) when I want Stripe/PostHog integrations. I can predict my bill."

## Signup Readiness
Score: 3/5 — "I'm intrigued enough to seriously consider it, but I'd hesitate. There's zero social proof on this page — no logos, no testimonials, no 'X founders use Foundry.' The claims are big ('12 agents run your company') and I have no evidence it works. I'd want a demo, a free trial, or at least a case study. The case studies link exists in the nav but I don't see it promoted on the landing page."

## First-Run Mental Model
Score: 3/5 — "I think I'd connect my GitHub repo, fill in some company DNA form, and then agents would start analyzing things. But I'm unclear on what happens in the first hour vs. first week. Do agents actually DO things (send emails, create PRs) or just advise? The briefing example shows agents 'closing security gaps automatically' which implies real actions, but the terms say 'analysis and recommendations.' Mixed signals."

## Would They Identify as Target User?
Yes — "The footer says 'SaaS Founders' and I'm a SaaS founder. The Solo plan is literally 'For solo founders.' I'm the target."

## Key Quote
"This looks like exactly what I need — an AI team running my business overnight — but I need proof it actually works before I hand over $79/month and my GitHub repo."
