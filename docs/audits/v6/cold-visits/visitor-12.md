# Cold Visitor 12 — Product Manager Evaluating Internal Tools

## First Impression (5 seconds)
"Connect your product. Get a company." — are we evaluating this as an internal tool? "Sovereign Company Protocol" is branding I don't understand. "12 specialized AI agents" — interesting, but I need to figure out if this fits our stack and process. The CTA says "Give my product a team" which implies this is for product owners, not PM teams.

## 30-Second Scan
The Compass agent does "Roadmap, lifecycle, feature priority" which overlaps with my role. Prism does "Onboarding, friction, activation" — also my territory. Would these agents augment my work or replace my job? The How It Works section assumes a solo founder connecting their product. In my world, there are stakeholders, sprints, Jira boards, product councils. This tool doesn't seem aware of existing product processes. The Growth tier integrates with Linear, which is relevant.

## Category Identification
Score: 3/5 — "It's an AI-powered virtual team for SaaS founders. Think of it as 'hire your first 12 employees, but they're AI.' It's not an internal tool platform or a product management tool. It's a founder tool that happens to include product-adjacent agents."

## Value Prop
Score: 2/5 — "My problem is evaluating tools for our team to use. Foundry is designed for a solo founder, not a product team. The Compass agent (roadmap/feature priority) could theoretically help me, but it's bundled with 11 other agents I don't need. I can't buy just the product intelligence piece. Also, this doesn't integrate with Jira or Notion — the Growth tier mentions Linear, but that's it."

## Pricing
Score: 3/5 — "Pricing is simple. $199/mo for Growth with integrations. But I'd be paying for 12 agents when I only want 2-3 product-relevant ones. Not efficient."

## Signup Readiness
Score: 1/5 — "No. This isn't the kind of tool I'm evaluating. It's a founder tool, not a team tool. I'd need it to integrate with our existing PM stack, support multiple team members with different permissions, and focus on product management specifically."

## First-Run Mental Model
Score: 2/5 — "I'd sign up and immediately feel like this isn't for me. The onboarding would ask me to be the 'founder' and connect 'my product.' I'm a PM; I don't own the company. The mental model assumes founder=user, which breaks for my context."

## Would They Identify as Target User?
No — "It says 'SaaS Founders.' I'm a PM at a SaaS company. Different persona entirely."

## Key Quote
"This is a founder tool, not a product tool. The Compass agent sounds useful in isolation but I can't unbundle it from the other 11 agents."
