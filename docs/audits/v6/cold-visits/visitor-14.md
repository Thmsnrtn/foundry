# Cold Visitor 14 — Agency Owner Managing 8 Client Products

## First Impression (5 seconds)
"Connect your product. Get a company." — I don't have a product; I manage 8 client products. "Sovereign Company Protocol" is meaningless to me. "12 specialized AI agents" — could these help me manage client deliverables? The page says "your SaaS product" (singular, possessive) — this assumes I own the product.

## 30-Second Scan
The agent roster is interesting but oriented around a founder running their own business. I need agents that help me manage client work: status reports, deliverable tracking, resource allocation. The agents here (CMO, CFO, CS) are business operation roles for a single company. I don't need a CMO for each client — I AM the outsourced marketing/dev team for them. The pricing caps at 5 companies on the $399 tier. I have 8 clients. No enterprise option.

## Category Identification
Score: 3/5 — "This is an AI virtual team for SaaS founders who own their product. It's not an agency management tool. The agents operate on YOUR business, not on client businesses you're servicing."

## Value Prop
Score: 1/5 — "Wrong audience. My problem is 'manage 8 client products efficiently.' This solves 'run your own SaaS with AI help.' Even if I could use it per-client, the agents would be advising on the client's business strategy — which isn't my role. I execute on deliverables, I don't set the client's marketing strategy."

## Pricing
Score: 2/5 — "$399/mo for 5 companies, but I have 8 clients. No way to scale beyond 5. And the per-company model doesn't match agency economics — I'd need to pass the cost to clients or absorb it. At $399 for 5 or ~$800 for 10 (if two plans), that's $80-100/client/month for AI agents my clients didn't ask for."

## Signup Readiness
Score: 1/5 — "Hard no. This doesn't fit the agency model. I'd need a white-label or client-management version where I could manage multiple client products from one agency dashboard."

## First-Run Mental Model
Score: 1/5 — "I'd sign up and immediately be confused about whose product I'm connecting. Mine? A client's? The onboarding assumes I'm the founder of the product. I'm not. I'm a service provider."

## Would They Identify as Target User?
No — "The footer says 'SaaS Founders.' I'm an agency owner. We're in completely different business models."

## Key Quote
"If they added an agency tier with white-label support and client management, I'd be very interested. But right now this is a founder tool and I'm not a founder."
