# Cold Visitor 17 — Corporate Innovation Team Lead

## First Impression (5 seconds)
"Sovereign Company Protocol" — sounds like something from our blockchain exploration phase. "Connect your product. Get a company." — we have internal products but they're not standalone companies. "12 specialized AI agents" — could be interesting for internal innovation projects. The design is clean and dark-themed, which is unusual for enterprise tools.

## 30-Second Scan

But this is designed for independent SaaS businesses, not for internal corporate products. It tracks MRR and churn — our internal products don't have revenue metrics. The "Company DNA" concept (ICP, positioning, voice) assumes a market-facing product with external customers.
