# Cold Visitor 05 — VC Partner Evaluating Portfolio Tools

## First Impression (5 seconds)
"Connect your product. Get a company." — unclear what this means in my context. I don't have a product; I invest in them. "Sovereign Company Protocol" sounds like crypto/web3 jargon. The subhead mentions "your SaaS product" and "12 specialized AI agents" — this is a founder tool, not a portfolio monitoring tool.

## 30-Second Scan
The 12-agent grid is interesting from an investment thesis perspective — this is ambitious. But it's a founder product. I'm evaluating whether to recommend this to portfolio companies or use it for portfolio oversight. The CEO briefing example shows individual company metrics (MRR, churn, signal score). If my portfolio companies used this, could I get read-only access to their briefings? Not mentioned. The Investor-Ready tier mentions "secure investor deal rooms with live Signal share" — that's closer to what I need but it's framed as a tool the founder uses to share with me, not something I control.

## Category Identification
Score: 3/5 — "This is an AI operating system for SaaS founders. It's in the 'AI agents for business' category, like a vertical AI team. It's not a portfolio monitoring tool for VCs, though the Investor-Ready tier has some investor-facing features."

## Value Prop
Score: 2/5 — "The value prop for founders is clear. The value prop for me as a VC is indirect: if my portfolio companies use this, I might get better visibility through deal rooms and signal sharing. But I can't see a VC use case from this landing page. There's no 'For Investors' section, no portfolio-level view, no way for me to monitor 20 companies from one dashboard."

## Pricing
Score: 3/5 — "Pricing is clear for founders ($79-$399). But my question is different: does each portfolio company pay separately? Is there a portfolio deal? An LP-facing dashboard? None of this is addressed. I'd need to talk to someone, but there's no 'Contact Sales' or 'For Investors' link."

## Signup Readiness
Score: 1/5 — "I wouldn't sign up. This isn't built for my use case. I might recommend it to a portfolio founder who's struggling with operations, but I'd want to see evidence first. No case studies, no logos, no metrics. I can't recommend an unproven tool to founders I've invested in."

## First-Run Mental Model
Score: 2/5 — "If I signed up, I'd get a founder experience — connect a product, see a briefing. But I don't have a product to connect. The product has no VC-specific onboarding path."

## Would They Identify as Target User?
No — "This is for SaaS founders. I'm a VC. The Investor-Ready tier is the closest thing to acknowledging my existence, but it's still built for the founder, not the investor."

## Key Quote
"Interesting product for founders, but I can't use it and I can't recommend it without proof. Show me a portfolio company that grew 30% faster with Foundry and we'll talk."
