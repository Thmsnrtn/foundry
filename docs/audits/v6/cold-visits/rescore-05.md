# Rescore 05 — Angel Investor Managing 12 Portfolio Companies

**Profile:** Angel investor with 12 active investments. Sits on 4 boards. Wants visibility into portfolio health without pestering founders for updates.

**Category ID (4/5):** "One control plane for all your companies" — I understand the vision. "Portfolio operators" card fits. But these aren't MY companies — I'm an investor, not a founder-operator. The page assumes I'm running the companies, not monitoring them from outside.

**Value Prop (3/5):** The portfolio briefing with signal scores and combined MRR across companies is interesting. But the 12 AI agents are designed to run operations (marketing, engineering, DevOps), which isn't my role. I want portfolio visibility, not operational control. The "investor deal rooms" in the $399 tier hint at my use case but aren't featured on the landing page.

**Pricing (3/5):** $399 for 5 companies when I have 12. Scale gap again. And I'm not sure the per-company agent model makes sense for an investor who wants dashboards, not autonomous agents running each company.

**Signup Readiness (2/5):** I'd bookmark it and forward to a few of my founders. The product seems built for them, not me. Unless there's an investor-specific mode where founders connect their companies and I get a read-only portfolio view — that's not clear from this page.

**First-Run Mental Model (3/5):** I'm confused about whether I'd ask each portfolio founder to connect their company, or whether I'd somehow add companies I don't operate. The onboarding assumes operational access (GitHub, Company DNA).

**Frame Alignment:** PARTIAL — I see "multi-company operating system" but realize I'm an adjacent persona (investor) rather than the core target (founder-operator).

**Key Quote:** "I'd forward this to my best founders, but I need an investor view — not operational control of companies I don't run."
