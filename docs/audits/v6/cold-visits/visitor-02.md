# Cold Visitor 02 — Multi-Product Founder (3 SaaS products)

## First Impression (5 seconds)
"Connect your product. Get a company." — singular. Immediately wonders: can I connect three products? The subhead mentions "your SaaS product" (singular again). Sees "12 specialized AI agents" and the team metaphor clicks, but the singular framing makes me feel this isn't built for my situation.

## 30-Second Scan
Scans the agent grid — interesting, 12 agents per product would be powerful. The "How It Works" section says "connect your product" (singular). Jumps to pricing. Solo plan: "1 company." Growth: doesn't mention multi-company. Investor-Ready: "Up to 5 companies (agent teams per company)." Found it — but that's $399/mo. For 3 products I need the most expensive tier. Feels like a jump from $79 to $399 just for multi-product.

## Category Identification
Score: 3/5 — "It's an AI operations platform for SaaS. But the hero messaging ('Sovereign Company Protocol,' 'Get a company') is confusing. Is this about AI agents or about company formation? The 12-agent team concept is clear once I scroll down, but the top-of-page positioning is muddy."

## Value Prop
Score: 3/5 — "The value prop makes sense for a solo-product founder, but for me with 3 products it's less clear. Can agents see across my products? Is there a portfolio view? The briefing example shows one product's metrics. I need cross-product intelligence — which product needs attention today? The 'Intelligence Network' on Growth tier sounds relevant but it says 'anonymized peer benchmarks,' not cross-portfolio."

## Pricing
Score: 3/5 — "I can predict my bill: $399/mo for up to 5 companies. But there's no middle option. Solo ($79) only supports 1 company. Growth ($199) doesn't mention multi-company at all — is it 1 company? The jump from 1 to 5 companies costs 5x more. I'd want a 3-company tier around $249. Also unclear: do I get 12 agents PER company at $399, or 12 total across companies?"

## Signup Readiness
Score: 2/5 — "Not signing up today. I need clarity on multi-product support, and $399/mo is a significant commitment without a trial. Also zero social proof. I'd bookmark it and maybe check back when there are case studies or a free tier to test with one product."

## First-Run Mental Model
Score: 3/5 — "I assume I'd set up each product separately with its own DNA and GitHub repo. But the page doesn't show me what managing multiple products looks like. Is there a portfolio dashboard? Can agents from Product A inform decisions about Product B? Unknown."

## Would They Identify as Target User?
Unsure — "The page says 'SaaS Founders' and I am one, but the entire page speaks in singular: 'your product,' 'a company.' Multi-product founders feel like an afterthought, only addressed in the pricing tier."

## Key Quote
"This is interesting for one of my products, but I can't tell if it actually understands multi-product founders or if it just lets you create separate silos at 5x the price."
