# Rescore 12 — Marketplace Founder (Not Pure SaaS)

**Profile:** Runs a two-sided marketplace ($40K GMV/mo). Has supply and demand sides. SaaS metrics don't cleanly apply — GMV, take rate, and liquidity matter more than MRR.

**Category ID (4/5):** "Operating System for Portfolio Founders" is clear. I understand the category — AI agents that run your business. The "one control plane for all your companies" makes sense as a frame, even though I only have one marketplace.

**Value Prop (3/5):** The 12 agents are modeled around SaaS ops: MRR tracking, churn, pricing experiments, GitHub code quality. My marketplace has supply-side ops, demand-side acquisition, matching algorithms, trust & safety, and GMV metrics. The briefing example shows MRR and churn — metrics that don't map to my business model. I'd wonder if the agents can adapt to marketplace dynamics.

**Pricing (4/5):** $79 Solo is reasonable to try. But if the agents are SaaS-tuned, I'm paying for capabilities that don't fit my model (e.g., Forge running "pricing experiments" when my pricing is a take rate on transactions).

**Signup Readiness (2/5):** I'd need evidence that Foundry works for non-SaaS models before committing. The Company DNA step ("ICP, positioning, voice, market insight") could theoretically accommodate a marketplace, but every concrete example on the page is SaaS. I'd look for a marketplace case study before signing up.

**First-Run Mental Model (3/5):** I'd connect my repo and fill in Company DNA. But I'd immediately wonder: do I define both sides of my marketplace? Does Harbor track supply-side retention separately from demand-side? The mental model assumes one product = one customer type.

**Frame Alignment:** YES — Correctly identifies "operating system for multi-company founders" as the category. But flags the SaaS-centric examples as a fit concern for marketplace models.

**Key Quote:** "The portfolio OS concept is strong, but every signal on this page says 'SaaS.' My marketplace doesn't speak MRR."
