# Cold Visitor 08 — Serial Entrepreneur (started and sold 5 companies)

## First Impression (5 seconds)
"Connect your product. Get a company." — I've built and sold 5 companies. The idea of "getting" a company via AI is amusing but intriguing. "12 specialized AI agents" — I've lived the pain of being the solo person doing all 12 of these roles. If this had existed 10 years ago, I would have killed for it.

## 30-Second Scan
The agent roster hits every pain point I've lived: technical debt (Atlas), positioning (Beacon), churn (Harbor), financial tracking (Ledger). The CEO briefing example is exactly the synthesis I always wished I had — instead of checking 6 dashboards every morning. The evolution engine where agents learn from corrections is smart; every company I've run had tribal knowledge that died when people left. The "How It Works" is simple: connect, activate, read briefings, agents improve.

## Category Identification
Score: 4/5 — "It's an AI-powered virtual executive team for SaaS products. The 'Sovereign Company Protocol' branding is a bit much — just call it what it is — but the core concept is crystal clear. It's in the 'AI business automation' category, specifically vertical for SaaS."

## Value Prop
Score: 4/5 — "If I were starting company #6 tomorrow, I'd be very interested. The value prop is: skip the first 6 hires and let AI handle operations while you focus on product and customers. The daily briefing alone would save me 2 hours/day of dashboard-checking. My only skepticism: I've seen a lot of 'AI will run your business' pitches and none have delivered. Where's the proof?"

## Pricing
Score: 4/5 — "$79/mo for a solo product is trivially cheap if it works. Even $399 for 5 companies is nothing compared to hiring. But 'if it works' is doing all the heavy lifting in that sentence. The pricing is clear and predictable — no surprises."

## Signup Readiness
Score: 3/5 — "I'd sign up if there were (a) a free trial, or (b) case studies from real founders. I've been burned by ambitious AI products before. The claims are big, the proof is zero. No testimonials, no logos, no 'founded by' credibility section. I'd google the founder before signing up."

## First-Run Mental Model
Score: 4/5 — "The How It Works section sets clear expectations: connect GitHub, fill in DNA, agents start working, read daily briefings. That's a clean mental model. I'd expect useful output within a few days. The trust/autonomy levels (agents start cautious, earn freedom) manage expectations well."

## Would They Identify as Target User?
Yes — "Absolutely. I am the exact target: a SaaS founder who knows the pain of doing everything alone. If I were building again, I'd try this on day one."

## Key Quote
"I've lived the problem this solves five times over. If the agents actually deliver, this is a no-brainer at $79/month. But I need proof before I trust my next company to it."
