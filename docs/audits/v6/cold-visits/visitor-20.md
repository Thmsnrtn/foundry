# Cold Visitor 20 — SaaS Founder Who Just Raised Series A

## First Impression (5 seconds)
"Connect your product. Get a company." — we already are a company, we just raised $3M. "12 specialized AI agents" — we're about to hire real people for these roles. Is this a bridge until we hire, or a replacement for hiring? "Sovereign Company Protocol" is confusing — sounds like web3 governance.

## 30-Second Scan
The agent grid is interesting. We just raised and need to hire a CMO, head of CS, and a data analyst. Could these agents buy us 3-6 months while we recruit? The CEO briefing would help me report to my new board. The Investor-Ready tier has "board packets — AI-drafted quarterly narratives" and "secure investor deal rooms" which is directly relevant post-raise. The "funding readiness score" is less relevant now (we just raised) but the board communication tools are.

## Category Identification
Score: 4/5 — "AI-powered virtual operations team for SaaS. Clear enough. It sits between 'AI assistant' and 'outsourced operations.' The 12-agent model with specific roles makes it easy to understand what you get."

## Value Prop
Score: 3/5 — "Mixed. Some agents solve an immediate problem (Beacon as interim CMO, Harbor as interim CS, Ledger for board reporting). But we're about to hire real people for these roles — will Foundry work alongside human hires, or does it assume the agents ARE the team? The landing page frames agents as the team, not as augmentation. At our stage, we need both: AI + human. That hybrid story isn't told here."

## Pricing
Score: 4/5 — "$399/mo for Investor-Ready with board packets and deal rooms. Post-Series A, that's trivially cheap. Compared to a $15K/mo CMO hire, $399 for a placeholder is nothing. Price is not a barrier. Clear tiers, no surprises."

## Signup Readiness
Score: 3/5 — "I'd consider the Investor-Ready tier for the board packet features alone. But I'd want to know: can agents work alongside my incoming CMO? Or do they get confused when two people are doing marketing strategy? The page doesn't address the 'agents + humans' collaboration model. I'd also want my co-founder and new hires to have access — Growth tier says 3 team members, Investor-Ready says unlimited, which is good."

## First-Run Mental Model
Score: 3/5 — "I'd sign up, connect our repo, fill in DNA, and start getting briefings. The board packet feature would be my primary use. But I'm worried about the transition: what happens when I hire a real CMO and Beacon's marketing advice conflicts with hers? The landing page doesn't prepare me for that scenario."

## Would They Identify as Target User?
Unsure — "I'm a SaaS founder, so yes on paper. But the page is designed for solo founders who don't have teams. I have funding and am about to hire. Am I supposed to use this temporarily? Permanently? The page doesn't tell me."

## Key Quote
"The board packet feature alone might be worth $399/mo post-raise. But I need to know: does this play nice with the human team I'm about to build, or is it designed for founders who never hire?"
