# Cold Visitor 16 — Small Business Consultant Managing 6 Clients

## First Impression (5 seconds)
"Connect your product. Get a company." — confusing. I'm a consultant, not a product builder. "12 specialized AI agents" — interesting but it says "your SaaS product." My clients are small businesses, some SaaS and some not. "Sovereign Company Protocol" means nothing to me.

## 30-Second Scan
The agent grid describes roles I partially fill for my clients: marketing strategy (Beacon), financial health (Ledger), analytics (Oracle). But this is specifically for SaaS — it mentions GitHub repos, code quality, MRR, churn rates. Half my clients are service businesses, not SaaS. The ones that are SaaS might benefit, but the tool is designed for the founder to use, not a consultant managing it on their behalf.

## Category Identification
Score: 3/5 — "AI business operations tool for SaaS founders. Not a consulting tool, not a client management platform. It's specifically SaaS-focused."

## Value Prop
Score: 2/5 — "For my SaaS clients, the agents could be useful. For my non-SaaS clients, irrelevant. And even for SaaS clients, I'd need to onboard them into a tool they manage, not one I manage for them. There's no consultant or advisor view. I can't log in and see all 6 clients' businesses."

## Pricing
Score: 2/5 — "Investor-Ready at $399/mo supports 5 companies. I have 6 clients, and only 3 are SaaS. The pricing doesn't account for my role as a consultant. Would each client pay separately? Would I pay for all of them? No guidance for my use case."

## Signup Readiness
Score: 1/5 — "Would not sign up. The product doesn't accommodate my consultant workflow. I might tell my SaaS clients about it, but I'd need to see it work first."

## First-Run Mental Model
Score: 1/5 — "I'd sign up and immediately be asked to connect 'my' product. But I don't have a product — I advise on other people's businesses. The onboarding would feel foreign."

## Would They Identify as Target User?
No — "SaaS Founders. I'm a small business consultant. Different world."

## Key Quote
"Cool concept for the right audience. I'm not that audience. My SaaS clients might be, but I can't easily bring them into this without a consultant-friendly model."
