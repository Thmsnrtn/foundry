## Conditions
Fleet size: 2
Tier: Growth ($199)
Device: iPad, Safari
Network: Home Wi-Fi

## Step-by-Step

1. **Jamie logs in at 9am** → Dashboard loads. Shows ScheduleWidget (last-used product via cookie). Signal: 68. Briefing card shows 1 pending decision. → Jamie reads the prose summary.
2. **Jamie checks the briefing** → Clicks "Briefing" in sidebar. Reads the CEO briefing for ScheduleWidget. Harbor flagged 2 churning customers. Beacon suggests a pricing change. → 2 minutes reading.
3. **Jamie resolves the pending decision** → Navigates to Decisions. One decision about pricing tier restructuring. Jamie reads the options, checks scenarios. Resolves it. → 3 minutes.
4. **Jamie needs to check InsightPulse** → Uses product switcher dropdown. Selects InsightPulse. Full page reload. → Dashboard loads for InsightPulse. Signal: 54. Lower than ScheduleWidget.
5. **Jamie reads InsightPulse briefing** → Clicks "Briefing" in sidebar. Briefing mentions a drop in activation rate. Oracle flagged a cohort retention concern. → 2 minutes.
6. **Jamie checks InsightPulse decisions** → Navigates to Decisions. 2 pending. Reviews and resolves both. → 5 minutes.
7. **Total triage: ~12 minutes for 2 products** → Acceptable, but Jamie had to fully context-switch between products. No unified view of "which product needs me most."
8. **Jamie's reaction** → "This took 12 minutes for 2 products. If I had 5, that's 30 minutes of clicking in and out. And I had to remember to check the second product — there was no alert telling me InsightPulse was struggling."

## Cross-Company Awareness

The product switcher change triggers a full page reload, and the intermediate state can be confusing.
