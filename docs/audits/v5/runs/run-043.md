# Run 043 — Riley x J08 (Settings & Billing Multi-Company)

## Conditions

| Axis | Value |
|------|-------|
| Persona | Riley (Growth-mode operator, 15 companies, 9/10 technical) |
| Journey | J08 — Settings and Billing with Multi-Company Pricing |
| Fleet size | F15 (15 companies) |
| Network | NET-FAST |
| Screen | SCR-DESK (1920x1080) |
| A11Y | A11Y-OFF |
| Account | ACCT-AGED (3+ months data) |
| Tier | TIER-INV ($399, Investor-Ready) |
| Error | ERR-NONE |

## Step-by-Step Transcript

1. Riley logs in. Portfolio view (15 companies). Navigates to Settings.
2. Settings loads. Subscription card: "Investor-Ready." No pricing shown on the page.
3. Products section: all 15 products listed vertically. On 1920x1080, this creates a long scrollable list.
4. **FRICTION**: Products list is not paginated or condensed. 15 products x ~40px per product = ~600px of product listings. Settings page becomes very long.
5. Riley scans for billing information. **None.** No amount, no next billing date, no payment method, no invoice link.
6. Riley clicks "Manage Subscription" -> Stripe portal.
7. Stripe portal: $399/month. Next billing: May 1. Payment: Visa ending 4242.
8. Riley returns. Wants to understand: "$399/mo for 15 companies = $26.60/company/month. Would it be cheaper to split into multiple accounts?" The UI provides no information to answer this.
9. Riley checks if there is a per-company add-on pricing structure. **No.** Flat $399 regardless of fleet size. 1 company or 15 — same price.
10. **FRICTION**: At 15 companies, Foundry is $26.60/company/month. At 1 company, it is $399/company/month. This pricing does not scale rationally. No volume discount, no per-company pricing, no tier that makes sense for 15 companies.
11. Riley wants to see per-company cost allocation for their holding company's accounting. **No cost allocation feature.**
12. Riley checks share links: Settings generates share tokens for `firstProduct` only. Riley needs share tokens for all 15 companies.
13. **FRICTION**: To generate share links for 15 companies, Riley would need to switch products 15 times and generate 15 tokens manually. And the Settings page `/settings/generate-share` always targets the first product anyway.
14. Riley checks the API. No programmatic way to generate share tokens or manage per-product settings.
15. Riley wants keyboard-driven bulk operations. **None available.** Every action requires mouse clicks.
16. Riley concludes that Settings does not scale to F15.

## Cross-Company Awareness Check

- **Product list**: All 15 companies visible and correctly named.
- **Subscription**: Founder-level, correctly displayed.
- **Per-product settings**: Broken — share link/ingest token always target first product.
- **No scaling**: Settings page was designed for 1-2 products and does not scale to 15.

## Friction Events

| # | Severity | Category | Description |
|---|----------|----------|-------------|
| F043-1 | HIGH | Scalability | Settings page does not scale to F15. Product list creates excessive vertical scrolling. No pagination, filtering, or search. |
| F043-2 | HIGH | Boundary Confusion | Share link and ingest token generation target first product regardless of selection. Cannot manage per-product settings at scale. |
| F043-3 | HIGH | Pricing Transparency | No per-company cost breakdown, no volume pricing, no cost allocation for multi-entity accounting. |
| F043-4 | MEDIUM | Missing Feature | No programmatic API for managing product settings (share tokens, ingest URLs). |
| F043-5 | MEDIUM | Missing Feature | No keyboard shortcuts for Settings operations. |

## Verdict

**FAILED**

Settings and billing do not scale to 15 companies. The page is a single-product design stretched to multi-product. Per-product settings are broken, billing is opaque, and there are no batch operations or API access for fleet-scale management.

## Recommendation

1. Redesign Settings for fleet scale: tabbed layout with Products, Billing, Integrations, Privacy sections.
2. Products section should be a searchable, sortable table with per-product actions (share link, ingest URL, retire, disconnect).
3. Add per-company cost allocation view for holding company accounting.
4. Provide API endpoints for product management operations.
5. Consider per-company add-on pricing above a base fleet count (e.g., $399 for up to 5 companies, +$49/additional company).
