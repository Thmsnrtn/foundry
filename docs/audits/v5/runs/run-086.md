# Run 086 — Casey (P08) x Cross-Company Intelligence (J06)

**Persona:** Casey — VP Engineering evaluating for startup studio adoption, Solo trial, would scale to 8-12 companies
**Journey:** Cross-company intelligence investigation
**Severity Baseline:** Casey needs to justify ROI for company-wide adoption. Fleet intelligence is a key selling point.

---

## Precondition Check

Casey has 1 company on Solo trial. Journey 06 requires 3+ companies. **BLOCKER: Fleet size = 1, tier = Solo.**

Casey's angle: this is an evaluation. Casey is asking "if we put 8-12 studio companies into Foundry, will we get cross-company intelligence?" The answer must be determinable from the marketing site, documentation, or trial experience.

---

## Simulation Transcript

### Step 1: Casey checks the pricing page for fleet intelligence claims

The landing page (`src/routes/public/landing.ts`) markets Foundry as a "multi-company autonomous control plane." Casey looks for specific claims about cross-company intelligence.

**Codebase evidence:** The landing page mentions portfolio management and multi-product support for Investor-Ready tier. But "cross-company intelligence" is not a named feature in the tier comparison or pricing table.

### Step 2: Casey adds a test company on Solo tier

Casey goes to `/onboarding` to add a product. The onboarding flow checks product count limits:

**Codebase evidence:** `src/routes/dashboard/onboarding.ts` lines 119, 232 — counts products where `status != 'archived'`. There is a tier-based limit check but the Solo tier allows at least 1 product. Whether it allows multiple depends on the tier gate configuration.

Casey tries to add a second product. The tier gate may block this — Solo tier is designed for single-company use.

### Step 3: Casey evaluates multi-product capabilities

Even if Casey upgrades to Growth or Investor-Ready to get multiple products, the evaluation reveals:
- Product switcher exists (cookie-based, `POST /switch-product`)
- Each product has independent SCP agents, dashboard, signal score
- No fleet overview that shows all products simultaneously
- No cross-company pattern extraction
- The portfolio system exists but is for investor entities, not founders

### Step 4: Casey looks for the evaluation checklist items

Casey's evaluation criteria:
1. **Multi-seat support** — Team mode exists (`/team`) but requires Growth tier at minimum. Roles are limited to co_founder, advisor, investor_observer. No "admin" or "viewer" or scoped permissions per company.
2. **SSO compatibility** — No SSO support. Auth is Clerk-based (email/password or Google OAuth). No SAML, no OIDC enterprise SSO.
3. **Admin console** — No admin console. No usage analytics per team member. No "who did what" across team.
4. **Onboarding friction** — Each product requires individual GitHub OAuth and repo analysis. For 8-12 products, this is 8-12 separate onboarding flows.

### Step 5: Casey calculates cost for 8-12 companies

At Investor-Ready ($399/mo), Casey gets all features including multi-product. But $399/mo is per-founder-account, not per-company. Is 1 account for 8-12 companies correct?

The pricing model is ambiguous: it is unclear whether 8-12 companies under one founder account costs $399/mo total or if there are per-company charges. Spoiler: there are no per-company charges. But this is not stated clearly.

**Casey's CTO presentation problem:** "It's $399/mo for unlimited companies" sounds too good to be true and raises questions about sustainability. "It's $399/mo per company" at 10 companies is $3,990/mo which is a harder sell. The pricing page does not clarify.

## Findings

| # | Severity | Finding |
|---|----------|---------|
| 1 | CRITICAL | **Cross-company intelligence does not exist** (same as Run 076). Casey cannot demonstrate fleet-level insights to their CTO. This is a deal-breaker for studio adoption. |
| 2 | CRITICAL | **No SSO / SAML support.** Enterprise evaluators like Casey require SSO for security review. Clerk provides email/password and social login but no enterprise SSO. |
| 3 | HIGH | **No admin console or usage analytics.** Casey cannot show the CTO "here's what the team is doing in Foundry" or "here's the ROI per company." |
| 4 | HIGH | **Team roles are limited.** Only co_founder, advisor, investor_observer. No "viewer" or per-company scoped access. Casey cannot invite the CTO to see results without giving them co-founder access. |
| 5 | HIGH | **Onboarding scales linearly.** Each of 8-12 products requires individual setup. No bulk import, no "connect 10 GitHub repos at once." |
| 6 | MEDIUM | **Pricing ambiguity for fleet scale.** Whether $399/mo covers unlimited companies or has per-company charges is not stated on the pricing page. |
| 7 | HIGH | **No trial experience of fleet features.** Solo tier Casey cannot experience multi-product features without paying. There is no sandbox or demo mode for fleet capabilities. |

## Cross-Company Awareness Check

Casey's studio evaluation surfaces the most important cross-company question: if Company A and Company B share customers, will Foundry detect the overlap and flag it? Answer: no. The products are completely isolated silos. Each has its own SCP, its own signal, its own decision queue. No cross-pollination, no correlation detection.

## Verdict: JOURNEY BLOCKED — Feature does not exist, and evaluation prerequisites (SSO, admin console, fleet view) are also missing
