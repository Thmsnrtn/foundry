# Run 019 — Riley (15-Company Power User) on Journey 04 (Daily Triage)

## Conditions
Fleet size: 15
Tier: Investor-Ready ($399)
Device: MacBook Pro, Arc browser
Network: Gigabit fiber

## Step-by-Step

1. **Riley opens Foundry at 7:30am** → Portfolio loads in ~3 seconds. 15 cards. Sorted by lowest Signal. Top 3 are in the 30s-40s range (critical). → Riley identifies the worst company: Company M (Signal 34).
2. **Riley clicks Company M** → Form POST, cookie set, redirect, dashboard loads. Signal 34, risk: RED. 6 pending decisions. 2 critical stressors. Briefing: "Revenue dropped 22% week-over-week. Customer churn accelerating." → This needs immediate attention. Riley reads briefing (2 min).
3. **Riley navigates to Decisions** → 6 decisions. Riley reviews each: the Decision Chamber opens in full-screen mode. No sidebar. → **FRICTION**: Each decision requires: click → read → resolve → click "All decisions" → click next. No "next decision" button. No keyboard shortcut to advance.
4. **After 12 minutes on Company M** → 6 decisions resolved. Riley needs to check 14 more companies. Types `/portfolio` in URL bar.
5. **Portfolio loads** → Riley clicks Company K (Signal 38). Switches, reads briefing, resolves 2 decisions. → 5 minutes.
6. **Back to portfolio (types URL)** → Company G (Signal 45). Briefing routine. 1 decision. → 3 minutes.
7. **After 40 minutes** → Riley has triaged 6 of 15 companies. 9 remain. The "green" companies (Signal 60+) still need a quick check each. → Riley estimates another 20 minutes for the remaining 9.
8. **Total triage: ~60 minutes** → For 15 companies. Riley's target: 15 minutes. Actual: 4x over budget.
9. **Riley's workaround** → Opens all 15 dashboards in separate tabs. Uses browser tab switching (Cmd+1 through Cmd+9, then clicks for 10-15). Scans each dashboard, closes tabs with no pending items. → This is faster than Foundry's own navigation: ~35 minutes.
10. **Riley's verdict** → "The tool is slower than browser tabs. I'm paying $399/mo to be slower."

## Cross-Company Awareness
Did the user get confused about which company they were acting on? **YES** — Twice. (1) After resolving Company M's 6th decision, Riley hit Escape expecting to return to portfolio. Instead, Escape closed the Chamber dialog and landed on Company M's dashboard. Riley started reading stressors thinking they were on Company K. (2) In the browser-tab workaround, Riley approved a decision on Company G's tab thinking it was Company F.

## Friction Events
- FRICTION-0061: CRITICAL — 60-minute triage for 15 companies. Target: 15 minutes. Foundry provides no sub-linear scaling tools: no unified decision queue, no fleet summary, no batch operations.
- FRICTION-0062: CRITICAL — No "next decision" navigation in the Decision Chamber. After resolving a decision, Riley must: close chamber → go to decision list → click next decision. Three clicks per decision. At 15+ decisions across 15 companies, that's 45+ unnecessary clicks.
- FRICTION-0063: CRITICAL — No keyboard shortcuts in Decision Chamber. Cannot press 'a' to approve, 'd' to dismiss, 'n' for next. Every action requires mouse interaction.
- FRICTION-0064: HIGH — No fleet-level briefing. Riley must read 15 separate briefings. A "fleet morning brief" summarizing all companies in one page would reduce reading time from 30 minutes to 5.
- FRICTION-0065: HIGH — Browser tabs are faster than Foundry's own navigation. The tool is negative-value for fleet-scale triage.

## Verdict: FAILED — triage takes 4x the target time, browser tabs outperform the tool
## Would Recommend: Never — "I want vim-style navigation. That's it. Instead I got a slideshow."
