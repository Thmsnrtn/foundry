# Run 095 — Devon (P09) x Full Org Deletion (J10)

**Persona:** Devon — blind founder, screen reader + keyboard user, Growth tier, 3 companies
**Journey:** Full organization deletion (churn path)
**Severity Baseline:** Devon must be able to safely execute the most destructive action entirely via keyboard/screen reader.

---

## Simulation Transcript

### Step 1: Devon decides to delete everything

Devon has 3 companies. Journey 10 requires deleting the entire organization. But the UI only supports per-product deletion. Devon must delete each product individually.

### Step 2: Devon navigates to the deletion flow for Company 1

Devon switches to Company 1 (product switcher, with auto-submit accessibility issue), navigates to `/privacy`, and finds the "Delete My Data" button.

### Step 3: Devon activates the "Delete My Data" button

VoiceOver reads: "Delete My Data, button." Devon activates it.

The delete modal opens: `document.getElementById('delete-modal').style.display='flex'`.

**CRITICAL ACCESSIBILITY FAILURE:**
1. **Focus does not move to the modal.** Devon's VoiceOver focus remains on the "Delete My Data" button. Devon has no announcement that a modal appeared.
2. **No `role="dialog"` on the modal.** VoiceOver does not know a dialog is open.
3. **No `aria-modal="true"`.** Background elements remain in the tab order. Devon could Tab past the modal into background content.
4. **No programmatic focus management.** The modal relies on visual attention (the overlay darkens the background) which is invisible to Devon.
5. **No Escape key handler.** Devon cannot dismiss the modal with Escape.

**Devon's experience:** Devon clicks "Delete My Data." Nothing appears to happen. The page seems unchanged. Devon presses Tab and navigates to background elements. The destructive modal is open but invisible to the screen reader.

### Step 4: Devon accidentally tabs into the "Yes, Delete Everything" button

If Devon tabs extensively, they may eventually reach the modal's buttons. But they have no context — VoiceOver would read "Cancel, button" and "Yes, Delete Everything, button" without the warning text that precedes them. Devon might click "Yes, Delete Everything" without knowing the full context.

Alternatively, Devon might never find the modal buttons and think the feature is broken.

### Step 5: Devon somehow completes deletion for Company 1

Assuming Devon eventually clicks "Yes, Delete Everything," the deletion is scheduled. The page redirects to `/privacy?success=deletion_scheduled`. VoiceOver reads the generic success banner but not a deletion-specific message.

### Step 6: Devon must repeat for Companies 2 and 3

Devon must:
1. Switch to Company 2 (product switcher auto-submit issue)
2. Navigate to `/privacy`
3. Activate "Delete My Data" (inaccessible modal)
4. Find and click "Yes, Delete Everything"
5. Repeat for Company 3

Three complete deletion flows, each with the inaccessible modal.

### Step 7: Devon checks for billing cancellation

Same finding as all J10 runs: billing is not cancelled by the deletion flow. Devon must separately manage the Stripe subscription.

### Step 8: Devon checks for deletion confirmation

No confirmation email. No deletion receipt. No accessible confirmation that data has been queued for deletion. Devon has no proof that the deletion was requested.

---

## Findings

| # | Severity | Finding |
|---|----------|---------|
| 1 | CRITICAL | **Delete modal is completely inaccessible to screen reader users.** No focus management, no dialog role, no aria-modal, no Escape key. Devon cannot safely or reliably use the most destructive feature in the product. |
| 2 | CRITICAL | **Risk of accidental deletion.** If Devon does reach the modal buttons via Tab, they arrive without the warning context. The destructive button has no additional confirmation challenge (type-to-confirm, re-authenticate). |
| 3 | CRITICAL | **No organization-level deletion.** Devon must delete 3 products individually, each requiring the inaccessible modal interaction. |
| 4 | HIGH | **Billing continues** (same as all J10 runs). |
| 5 | HIGH | **No accessible confirmation of deletion.** No email receipt, no screen-reader-friendly confirmation page, no timestamp proof. |
| 6 | HIGH | **Inconsistent modal patterns.** The Signal Anatomy dialog uses native `<dialog>` (accessible). The delete confirmation uses a styled `<div>` (inaccessible). The most destructive action uses the least accessible pattern. |

## Cross-Company Awareness Check

Devon has 3 companies. Per-product deletion ensures no cross-company impact — deleting Company A does not affect Company B. Good isolation. But the per-product deletion model means Devon must interact with the inaccessible modal 3 times. And the lack of org-level deletion means Devon cannot clean-exit with a single action.

## Verdict: JOURNEY FAILED — Deletion is inaccessible. Screen reader users cannot safely complete the most destructive action in the product.
