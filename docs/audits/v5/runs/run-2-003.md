## Step-by-Step (against current code)

1. Robin navigates to Privacy & Data. Page loads with product name in context.
2. Section 3 "Your Data" shows: "Export Current Product" (JSON/CSV), "Request Product Deletion" with product-specific button.
3. Robin clicks "Delete This Product". Modal opens with `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, `aria-label="Confirm product deletion"`. Modal says: "Delete all data for [Robin's Product]?" with specific product name.
4. Modal explains: "This will permanently schedule deletion of all data for **[ProductName]** including metrics, briefings, decisions, and agent logs."
5. Modal offers export link before proceeding. Robin can export first.
6. Robin clicks "Yes, Delete [ProductName]". POST to `/privacy/delete` calls `scheduleDataDeletion(productId, 30)`.
7. Redirect to `/privacy?success=deletion_scheduled`. Banner says: "Deletion scheduled. Product data will be removed after 30 days."
8. Behind the scenes: `data_deletion_processor` job runs daily. After 30 days, `processScheduledDeletions` executes — deletes across 14 tables, sets `github_access_token = NULL`, sets product status to 'deleted'.
