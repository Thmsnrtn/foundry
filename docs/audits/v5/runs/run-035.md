## Step-by-Step Transcript

1. Jamie logs in. Frustrated with Foundry. Wants to delete everything and cancel.
2. Dashboard redirects to portfolio gate. Jamie navigates to `/settings`.
3. Jamie does not see a "Delete Account" or "Close Account" option on Settings.
4. Jamie navigates to Privacy & Data (`/privacy`).
5. Jamie sees "Delete My Data" button. Reads: "Permanently delete your product and all associated data."
6. **FRICTION**: "your product" — singular. Jamie has 2 products. Does this delete one or both?
7. Jamie clicks "Delete My Data." Modal appears: "This will permanently schedule deletion of all your product data."
8. **CRITICAL**: "all your product data" is ambiguous. Does "your product" mean the currently selected product or all products? The code targets `ctx.productId` — one product only.
9. Jamie clicks "Yes, Delete Everything." POST to `/privacy/delete`.
10. Redirect with success banner. Jamie thinks everything is deleted.
11. Jamie navigates to Dashboard. Gets redirected to portfolio gate. Navigates to Settings.
12. **Settings still shows 2 products.** The first product has a deletion scheduled but appears active. The second product was never touched.
13. Jamie is confused: "I clicked 'Delete Everything' but both products are still here."
14. **FRICTION**: Jamie needs to understand that "Delete Everything" only means "delete everything for this one product." But the modal language says "all your product data" which Jamie interprets as "all of my data."
15. Jamie would need to: switch to the second product, navigate to Privacy, and delete again. Then deal with the Stripe subscription separately.
16. But Jamie cannot switch products (portfolio gate issue). And Stripe is not cancelled by the delete action.
17. Jamie gives up and emails support. **The journey requires contacting support** — one of the abandonment criteria for J10.
18. **Post-30-days (simulated)**: Only one product's data is deleted. The other product, the founder account, and the Stripe subscription persist.

## Cross-Company Awareness Check

- **CRITICAL**: "Delete Everything" deletes one product. Jamie interprets it as account-wide. The modal language is misleading for multi-product founders.
- **No account deletion path**: The `deletion_requests` table and `processAccountDeletions` job exist but no UI creates deletion request records.
- **Billing persists**: Stripe charges continue after "deletion."

## Verdict

**FAILED**

Jamie cannot achieve full organization deletion. The "Delete Everything" flow is misleadingly scoped to a single product, billing is not cancelled, and the second product remains active. Jamie must contact support to complete the churn path.

## Recommendation

1. Create a distinct "Close Account" flow on the Settings page that: cancels Stripe, stops all SCP agents, schedules deletion for ALL products, and enters a 30-day grace period.
2. Rename "Delete Everything" to "Delete This Product's Data" with the product name shown.
3. The Close Account flow should use the existing `deletion_requests` table and trigger `processAccountDeletions`.
4. Send a cancellation confirmation email with a timeline and reactivation instructions.
