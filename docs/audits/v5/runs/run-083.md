# Run 083 — Taylor (P07) x Settings & Billing Multi-Company (J08)

**Persona:** Taylor — returning user after 6-week absence, Growth tier, 1 company
**Journey:** Settings and billing with multi-company pricing
**Severity Baseline:** Taylor has been charged for 6 weeks while absent. What does billing look like?

---

## Simulation Transcript

### Step 1: Taylor checks if they were charged during absence

Taylor goes to `/settings`. The billing section shows the Growth tier badge and "Manage Subscription" button. No billing history visible. No "you were charged $199 on March 15 and $199 on April 15" summary.

Taylor clicks "Manage Subscription" to go to Stripe's portal. In the Stripe portal, Taylor can see the two invoices charged during absence. But this is Stripe's UI, not Foundry's.

### Step 2: Taylor evaluates whether the agents justified the cost

Taylor was charged $398 (2 x $199) while away. The agents ran for 6 weeks unsupervised. Did they produce value? Taylor has no way to assess this from the billing page.

A "cost vs. value" view is not available. Journey 08 expects "usage" visibility alongside billing. No usage metrics exist on the settings page — no "agents ran X times," "Y decisions auto-approved," "Z signals generated."

### Step 3: Taylor considers downgrading

Taylor might want to downgrade to Solo ($79) given limited engagement. The settings page shows "Upgrade to Investor Ready" but no downgrade button. Downgrading requires going through the Stripe portal.

**Codebase evidence:** `src/routes/dashboard/settings.ts` lines 82-93 — the UI conditionally shows upgrade buttons but never shows a downgrade button. Downgrade is only available via "Manage Subscription" (Stripe portal).

### Step 4: Taylor checks feature access after potential downgrade

No preview of what features would be lost on downgrade. No "you would lose access to: team mode, wisdom layer, advanced integrations" summary.

### Step 5: Taylor checks multi-company pricing (hypothetical)

Same findings as Run 078 — pricing is flat per-tier, not per-company. Taylor has one product so this is not immediately relevant, but if Taylor considered adding a second product, there is no pricing guidance on the settings page.

---

## Findings

| # | Severity | Finding |
|---|----------|---------|
| 1 | HIGH | **No in-app billing history.** Taylor cannot see charges during absence without leaving Foundry to visit Stripe portal. |
| 2 | HIGH | **No usage summary alongside billing.** No "here's what you got for your $199/month" — no agent run counts, decision throughput, or value metrics. |
| 3 | MEDIUM | **No downgrade option in UI.** Only upgrade buttons shown. Downgrade requires Stripe portal navigation. |
| 4 | MEDIUM | **No feature loss preview on downgrade.** Taylor cannot assess what they would lose before downgrading. |
| 5 | HIGH | **6-week absence cost visibility.** Taylor paid $398 during absence with no value summary. This is a churn trigger for returning users. |

## Cross-Company Awareness Check

Not applicable — single company, flat-rate billing.

## Verdict: JOURNEY PARTIAL — Billing works (Stripe charges correctly) but transparency and in-app management fail
