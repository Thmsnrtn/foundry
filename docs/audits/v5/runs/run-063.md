## Preconditions

Riley operates 12 companies on Investor-Ready. Hypothetical scenario: Riley's startup studio is shutting down and needs to delete the entire organization -- all 12 companies, all data, cancel billing. Riley needs a complete, verifiable exit.

## Transcript

### Step 1: Find org-level deletion

Riley looks for an organization or account-level deletion option that covers all 12 companies at once.

**Codebase finding:** The Privacy page (`privacy.ts`) has "Delete My Data" which calls `scheduleDataDeletion(ctx.productId, 30)` -- this deletes a SINGLE product's data. There is no organization-level or fleet-level deletion. Riley would need to switch to each of the 12 companies and delete each one individually.

There is no `/account/delete` or `/org/delete` endpoint in the routes.

**Friction:** Deleting 12 companies requires 12 separate deletion actions, each requiring: (1) switch product, (2) navigate to Privacy, (3) click Delete My Data, (4) confirm in modal. That's ~48 interactions for a full fleet deletion.

**Result:** FRICTION (CRITICAL) -- No fleet-level or account-level deletion. Must delete each company individually. At 12 companies, this is 48+ interactions.

### Step 2: Verify data completeness before deletion

Riley wants to export all data before deletion -- a complete data archive across all 12 companies.

**Codebase finding:** The export at `/privacy/export` is per-product. There is no fleet-wide export. Riley must download 12 separate JSON files.

**Result:** FRICTION (HIGH) -- No fleet-wide export. Must export each company individually.

### Step 3: Execute first company deletion

Riley switches to company 1 and initiates deletion.

**Codebase finding:** privacy.ts line 291-310 shows the modal. The `scheduleDataDeletion` function schedules deletion with a 30-day delay.

**After deletion, Riley is redirected to `/privacy?success=deletion_scheduled` with a "Settings saved successfully" message. The current product's data is now scheduled for deletion. But the sidebar and navigation still show the product. The cookie still references this product. What happens when Riley tries to navigate?

**Friction:** After scheduling deletion, the product is presumably still in the `products` table (not yet deleted). The `getProductsByOwner` query in `_shared.ts` will still return it. The product will continue to appear in the product switcher until the 30-day deletion actually executes. This creates a zombie state -- the product appears active but its data is scheduled for deletion.

**Result:** FRICTION (HIGH) -- Deleted product remains in navigation and product switcher during the 30-day deletion window. No visual indication that deletion is scheduled.

### Step 4: Repeat for remaining companies

Riley must repeat the deletion process for the remaining 11 companies.

**Result:** FRICTION (CRITICAL, duplicate of Step 1) -- O(n) deletion process.

### Step 5: Cancel subscription

After deleting all companies, Riley navigates to Stripe to cancel the $399/month subscription.

**Codebase finding:** Same as run-061 finding F-061-D. Billing cancellation is in Stripe, not in Foundry. There is no prompt to cancel billing after all products are deleted.

**Friction:** After deleting 12 companies, Riley still needs to navigate to Stripe separately to cancel billing. Foundry does not auto-cancel or prompt for billing cancellation when the last product is deleted.

**Result:** FRICTION (HIGH) -- No billing cancellation prompt after fleet-wide deletion.

### Step 6: Cross-company boundary check

During the serial deletion process, Riley switches between companies. Each switch shows the correct company's data.

**Cross-company awareness check:** PASS. Product isolation is maintained during the deletion workflow. Deleting Company A does not affect Company B's data or status.

**Result:** PASS -- Boundary maintained during serial deletion.
