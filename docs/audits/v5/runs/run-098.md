# Run 098 — Robin (P10) x Settings & Billing Multi-Company (J08)

**Persona:** Robin — frustrated user about to churn, Growth tier, 1 company
**Journey:** Settings and billing with multi-company pricing
**Severity Baseline:** Robin had two billing issues go unresolved for weeks. Billing is a sore point.

---

## Simulation Transcript

### Step 1: Robin navigates to billing with pre-existing frustration

Robin's backstory includes "two billing issues went unresolved for weeks." This means Robin has already tried to manage billing and found the experience inadequate.

Robin goes to `/settings`. The billing section shows Growth tier and "Manage Subscription" button.

### Step 2: Robin wants to see billing history in Foundry

Robin wants to verify they were not double-charged or incorrectly billed during the period when they had 2 products. No billing history exists in Foundry. Robin must go to Stripe's portal.

### Step 3: Robin clicks "Manage Subscription" to investigate billing issues

Robin is redirected to Stripe's billing portal. In the portal, Robin can see:
- Current subscription
- Past invoices
- Payment history

But Stripe's portal does not have Foundry-specific context. It shows "Foundry Growth Plan - $199.00" but does not break down what the charge covered (which products, which features used, which agents ran).

### Step 4: Robin considers downgrading before cancelling

Robin might want to downgrade from Growth ($199) to Solo ($79) as a last resort before cancelling entirely. The settings page has no downgrade button — only "Upgrade to Investor Ready."

To downgrade, Robin must navigate Stripe's portal and change the subscription there (if Stripe's portal is configured to allow plan changes). But Stripe's portal may not show Foundry's tier names or pricing clearly.

### Step 5: Robin checks if billing adjusted when the second company was removed

Robin previously removed one company. The bill did not change ($199/mo Growth). Robin expected it might decrease. It did not because pricing is flat per-tier.

**Robin's reaction:** "I'm paying the same whether I have 1 product or 2. So removing one product saved me nothing. What am I paying for?"

This is a fundamental value communication failure. Robin cannot see what the $199/mo buys at the per-company level.

### Step 6: Robin looks for a billing support channel

No billing support option exists in the settings page. No "contact us about billing" link. No in-app support chat. Robin would need to email support manually.

The persona says Robin's two billing issues "went unresolved for weeks" — this implies Robin tried to get help and failed. The product has no self-service billing dispute resolution and no visible support escalation path.

---

## Findings

| # | Severity | Finding |
|---|----------|---------|
| 1 | CRITICAL | **No in-app billing history or dispute resolution.** Robin had billing issues that went unresolved for weeks. The product offers zero self-service billing investigation tools. |
| 2 | HIGH | **No downgrade path in Foundry UI.** Only upgrade buttons shown. Downgrade requires navigating Stripe's external portal. |
| 3 | HIGH | **Flat-rate pricing creates value perception problem.** Robin removed a product but the bill did not change. This reinforces the feeling of paying for nothing. |
| 4 | HIGH | **No support channel visible.** No "contact us," no billing help, no in-app support chat on the settings page. Robin's billing issues went unresolved because the escalation path is invisible. |
| 5 | MEDIUM | **Stripe portal lacks Foundry context.** The portal shows Stripe plan names and amounts but not Foundry-specific usage data, product associations, or agent activity metrics. |
| 6 | HIGH | **Billing frustration is a direct churn driver.** Robin explicitly cites unresolved billing issues as part of their decision to leave. The product does nothing to address this on the settings page. |

## Cross-Company Awareness Check

Robin went from 2 products to 1. Billing did not change. No cross-company billing boundary to violate because pricing is flat per-tier. But the lack of per-company value attribution means Robin cannot assess whether either product justified the cost.

## Verdict: JOURNEY FAILED — Billing management is Stripe-delegated with no in-app investigation, no downgrade path, and no support channel. Directly contributes to churn.
