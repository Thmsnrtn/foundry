# Run 078 — Jordan (P06) x Settings & Billing Multi-Company (J08)

**Persona:** Jordan — compliance-minded fintech founder, Growth tier, 1 company
**Journey:** Settings and billing with multi-company pricing
**Severity Baseline:** Jordan cares about billing transparency for audit/compliance purposes.

---

## Simulation Transcript

### Step 1: Jordan navigates to Settings > Billing

Jordan goes to `/settings`. The billing section shows:
- Current Plan: Growth badge
- Feature count summary
- "Upgrade to Investor Ready" button
- "Manage Subscription" button (links to Stripe Customer Portal)

**No per-company cost breakdown.** The card shows tier name and feature count but not the price ($199/mo) or next billing date.

**Codebase evidence:** `src/routes/dashboard/settings.ts` lines 77-93 — the subscription card shows `tierLabel` and `capabilities.length` but never renders the actual dollar amount or billing cycle date.

### Step 2: Jordan tries to preview tier change pricing

There is no tier change preview. The only options are:
- Click "Upgrade to Investor Ready" (redirects to Stripe Checkout immediately)
- Click "Manage Subscription" (redirects to Stripe Customer Portal)

No inline simulation of "what would my bill look like if I changed tiers." No proration calculator. No before/after comparison.

**Codebase evidence:** `src/routes/dashboard/settings.ts` — the checkout POST handler creates a Stripe session immediately. No preview step.

### Step 3: Jordan clicks "Manage Subscription"

This creates a Stripe Billing Portal session and redirects. The Stripe portal handles invoices, payment methods, and cancellation — but it is Stripe's UI, not Foundry's. Jordan cannot access billing history within Foundry itself.

**Codebase evidence:** `src/routes/dashboard/settings.ts` lines 295-315 — the handler creates a `stripe.billingPortal.sessions.create()` and redirects. Zero in-app billing history.

### Step 4: Jordan checks for invoice downloads

No invoice download feature exists in Foundry. Stripe's portal may offer invoice PDFs, but Foundry itself has no `/billing/invoices` route and no invoice rendering.

### Step 5: Jordan examines multi-company pricing model

The pricing model is flat per-tier, not per-company:
- Solo: $79/mo (single company implied)
- Growth: $199/mo
- Investor-Ready: $399/mo

There is no per-company pricing component. Adding a second company does not increase the bill. Removing a company does not decrease it. The Journey 08 expectation of "per-company breakdown" and "real-time billing updates when adding/removing companies" is not applicable to this pricing model.

**Codebase evidence:** `src/services/billing/stripe.ts` uses static Stripe Price IDs per tier. No metered billing, no per-company line items.

### Step 6: Jordan as compliance founder wants a billing audit trail

Jordan needs to show their auditor a clear record of subscription changes, charges, and tier transitions. None of this exists within Foundry. The audit log (`/audit-log`) tracks agent actions, not billing events. Stripe webhooks update the tier but do not log to the audit log.

**Codebase evidence:** `src/services/integrations/stripe-webhook.ts` processes `checkout.session.completed` to update `founders.tier` but does not write an audit log entry.

---

## Findings

| # | Severity | Finding |
|---|----------|---------|
| 1 | HIGH | **No in-app billing details.** The settings page shows tier name and feature count but not price, billing date, or payment method. Everything is deferred to Stripe's external portal. |
| 2 | HIGH | **No tier change preview.** Clicking "Upgrade" goes directly to Stripe Checkout with no proration preview, no before/after comparison, no "are you sure" step within Foundry. |
| 3 | HIGH | **No invoice history in Foundry.** Invoice access requires navigating to Stripe's external billing portal. No in-app invoice list or PDF download. |
| 4 | MEDIUM | **Pricing is flat per-tier, not per-company.** Journey 08 assumes per-company pricing that scales with fleet size. The actual model is flat-rate tiers. This is a product model mismatch, not a bug, but it means "adding/removing companies updates billing in real-time" is not true. |
| 5 | HIGH | **No billing audit trail.** Stripe webhook tier updates are not logged to `agent_audit_log`. Jordan cannot demonstrate subscription change history to an auditor. |
| 6 | MEDIUM | **Stripe portal redirect loses context.** After managing billing in Stripe's portal, the return URL is `/settings` with no success/failure indication about what was changed. |

## Cross-Company Awareness Check

Not applicable for this run — pricing is flat per-tier, so there is no cross-company billing boundary to violate. However, if per-company pricing were added, the current Stripe integration has no mechanism to track company count or adjust billing dynamically.

## Verdict: JOURNEY PARTIAL — Billing exists via Stripe delegation but fails transparency, preview, and audit trail requirements
