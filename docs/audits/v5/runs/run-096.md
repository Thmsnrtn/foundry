# Run 096 — Robin (P10) x Cross-Company Intelligence (J06)

**Persona:** Robin — frustrated user about to churn, Growth tier, 1 company (down from 2)
**Journey:** Cross-company intelligence investigation
**Severity Baseline:** Robin has zero trust in agents. Previously had 2 companies, now 1. Already partially exited.

---

## Precondition Check

Robin has 1 company (down from 2 — already removed one). Journey 06 requires 3+ companies. **BLOCKER: Fleet size = 1.**

Robin's angle is retrospective: Robin previously had 2 companies and never saw any cross-company intelligence. This absence may have contributed to the perception that Foundry's value proposition was not being delivered.

---

## Simulation Transcript

### Step 1: Robin's history with multi-product

Robin previously had 2 products. During that time:
- Each product had independent SCP, dashboard, and signal
- The product switcher (cookie-based) allowed toggling between them
- No cross-company insights, correlations, or fleet-level recommendations ever appeared
- Agent recommendations became repetitive across both products

Robin removed one product (via... what mechanism? The only option is deletion via privacy page). The removed product's data was either deleted or is inaccessible.

### Step 2: Robin reflects on the value gap

Robin's complaint: "The decision queue felt like busywork." Cross-company intelligence could have addressed this by showing "both your products have the same churn pattern — here's one fix that helps both." Instead, each product's agents independently recommended similar actions, doubling the decision queue workload without compounding the insight.

### Step 3: Robin attempts the journey

Robin navigates the dashboard. No fleet intelligence. No cross-company view. No historical reference to the removed second product.

With 1 company remaining and zero trust in the agents, Robin has no reason to explore fleet features even if they existed.

---

## Findings

| # | Severity | Finding |
|---|----------|---------|
| 1 | CRITICAL | **Cross-company intelligence does not exist** (same as all J06 runs). |
| 2 | HIGH | **Repetitive agent recommendations across products contributed to churn.** Without cross-company deduplication, two products generating overlapping recommendations feels like busywork, not intelligence. This is a direct cause of Robin's frustration. |
| 3 | MEDIUM | **No trace of removed product.** Robin removed one product and it vanished completely. No "previously you had 2 products" acknowledgment. No historical reference. |
| 4 | HIGH | **The absence of fleet intelligence is a churn driver.** Robin had the ideal setup for cross-company value (2 products) and received zero cross-company intelligence. The feature gap directly contributed to the decision to leave. |

## Cross-Company Awareness Check

Robin's 2-product history is the simplest cross-company test case. With only 2 products, cross-company intelligence is "trivial" (per Journey 06 description) but still absent. The `decision_patterns` table may have captured patterns from Robin's removed product that still influence the remaining product — patterns from a product Robin chose to remove.

**Boundary concern:** Patterns from Robin's deleted product may persist in the global pool and continue to influence Robin's remaining product and other founders' products.

## Verdict: JOURNEY BLOCKED — Feature does not exist. Fleet intelligence absence contributed to churn.
