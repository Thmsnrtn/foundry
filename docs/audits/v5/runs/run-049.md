## Verdict

**FAILED**

Morgan identifies the compliance export as fundamentally non-compliant with GDPR. The self-service export is incomplete, consent defaults violate Article 7, and personal data is not included. Two separate export codepaths with different coverage levels indicate a design-level defect.

## Recommendation

1. Unify the export codepaths: route the self-service export through the comprehensive GDPR job.
2. Change consent defaults to OFF (opt-in instead of pre-ticked). Per GDPR Article 7, silence or pre-ticked boxes do not constitute valid consent.
3. Include founder personal data in the export (name, email, preferences, consent records).
4. Add a manifest: list of all data categories, included/excluded status, exclusion justifications.
5. Add export versioning and checksums for audit trail integrity.
