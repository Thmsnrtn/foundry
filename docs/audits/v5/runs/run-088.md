# Run 088 — Casey (P08) x Settings & Billing Multi-Company (J08)

**Persona:** Casey — VP Engineering evaluating for startup studio, Solo trial
**Journey:** Settings and billing with multi-company pricing
**Severity Baseline:** Casey is spending someone else's budget. Per-dollar-per-company ROI must be demonstrable.

---

## Simulation Transcript

### Step 1: Casey evaluates pricing for 8-12 companies

Casey's evaluation task: "How much will this cost for our studio's 8-12 products?"

The pricing page shows three tiers: Solo ($79), Growth ($199), Investor-Ready ($399). Multi-product support requires Investor-Ready tier.

**Question Casey cannot answer from the pricing page:** Is $399/mo for all 12 products, or per-product? The pricing page does not specify per-company charges. Casey would need to sign up and try to add products to discover the model.

### Step 2: Casey signs up for Solo and tries to model scaling

Casey has one product on Solo trial. To see multi-product pricing, Casey would need to upgrade. But Casey is evaluating — not ready to commit budget.

**No pricing calculator or simulation tool.** Casey cannot model "12 products on Investor-Ready = $X/month" without contacting sales or guessing.

### Step 3: Casey checks the settings page for billing management

Same findings as Run 078: minimal in-app billing, deferred to Stripe portal.

Casey's specific concerns:
1. **Can I see per-company cost breakdown?** No — billing is per-tier, no per-company line items.
2. **Can I add/remove companies and see billing update?** Adding companies does not change billing. No per-company pricing exists.
3. **Can I download invoices for accounting?** Only via Stripe portal.
4. **Can I set up a company credit card for the team?** Payment is on the founder's personal Stripe account. No team billing or PO support.

### Step 4: Casey evaluates admin billing controls

Casey needs:
- Centralized billing for the studio
- Per-company cost allocation for internal chargeback
- Invoice downloads for expense reporting
- Budget alerts when usage exceeds thresholds

None of these exist. Billing is single-founder-account Stripe subscription.

### Step 5: Casey checks for enterprise / volume pricing

No enterprise tier. No volume discounts. No "contact sales" for custom pricing. The highest tier is $399/mo.

For a studio running 12 products at $399/mo total, this is actually affordable ($33/company/month). But Casey has no way to confirm this is the pricing model without trial and error.

---

## Findings

| # | Severity | Finding |
|---|----------|---------|
| 1 | CRITICAL | **Pricing clarity for multi-company is absent.** Casey cannot determine the cost for 8-12 companies from the pricing page. The flat-rate model is actually favorable but is not communicated. |
| 2 | HIGH | **No team/enterprise billing.** Single-person Stripe subscription with no PO, no team billing, no cost center allocation. Enterprise evaluators require these. |
| 3 | HIGH | **No per-company cost attribution.** Casey cannot generate internal reports showing "Company A costs $X, Company B costs $Y." |
| 4 | HIGH | **No pricing calculator.** Casey cannot model fleet-scale pricing without signing up and experimenting. |
| 5 | MEDIUM | **No enterprise tier or volume discounts.** For studio adoption at 12+ companies, Casey would expect a volume discount or enterprise agreement. |
| 6 | MEDIUM | **No invoice access within Foundry.** Expense reporting requires navigating to Stripe's external portal. |

## Cross-Company Awareness Check

Billing has no cross-company boundary issue because pricing is flat per-tier. One payment covers all companies. However, the lack of per-company attribution means Casey cannot track which products are active/valuable and which should be retired from a cost perspective.

## Verdict: JOURNEY PARTIAL — Billing functions (Stripe charges correctly) but enterprise evaluation requirements (clarity, attribution, team billing) fail
