# Run 014 — Sam (5-Company Portfolio) on Journey 04 (Daily Triage)

## Conditions
Fleet size: 5
Tier: Investor-Ready ($399)
Device: MacBook Pro, Firefox
Network: Office broadband

## Step-by-Step

1. **Monday 8:00am — Sam opens Foundry** → Lands on `/portfolio` (no product cookie set; last session cleared). 5 cards visible sorted by Signal. Company E is lowest (41) — Sam knows to check that first.
2. **Sam clicks Company E** → Form POST to `/switch-product` → redirect → Dashboard loads. Signal 41, risk state: YELLOW. 3 critical stressors. 4 pending decisions. Briefing headline: "Revenue contraction accelerated. Churn rate doubled this week." → This is serious. Sam reads the full briefing (3 minutes).
3. **Sam reviews decisions for Company E** → 4 decisions pending. Reviews each in the Chamber (full-screen). Resolves 3, defers 1 that needs more data. → 8 minutes.
4. **Sam wants to go back to portfolio** → No "back to fleet" link in sidebar or header. The logo links to `/dashboard` (current product). → Sam types `/portfolio` in the URL bar. Portfolio loads.
5. **Sam clicks Company D (Signal 56)** → Dashboard loads after product switch. Signal 56, risk: GREEN. 1 pending decision. → Quick. Reads briefing (1 min), resolves decision (2 min).
6. **Back to portfolio (manually)** → Types URL again. Clicks Company B. Dashboard loads. Signal 63. No pending decisions. Briefing is routine. → 1 minute.
7. **Back to portfolio** → Company C: Signal 69. No pending decisions. → 30 seconds.
8. **Back to portfolio** → Company A: Signal 71. 1 pending decision. → Resolves it. 2 minutes.
9. **Total triage time: ~28 minutes** → For 5 companies. This should be 15 minutes max. The overhead is navigation: 5 portfolio-loads + 5 product switches + multiple sidebar navigations within each product.
10. **Sam's assessment** → "28 minutes for 5 companies. At this rate, 15 companies would take over an hour. This is slower than my spreadsheet."

## Cross-Company Awareness
Did the user get confused about which company they were acting on? **YES** — After resolving a decision for Company D, Sam hit the browser back button expecting to return to portfolio. Instead it loaded the Decision Chamber for the just-resolved decision. Sam then clicked "All decisions" which showed Company D's empty decision queue. Sam had to mentally track which company was active via the header dropdown. At one point, Sam started typing in the Ask bar thinking they were on Company E but were actually on Company B.

## Friction Events
- FRICTION-0041: CRITICAL — No "back to fleet/portfolio" navigation anywhere in the product-scoped UI. Sam must manually type `/portfolio` or use browser navigation. This breaks the triage flow every time.
- FRICTION-0042: CRITICAL — No cross-company decision queue. Sam reviewed 6 decisions across 5 companies, each requiring a product switch → decisions page → decision detail → resolve → back. A unified queue would collapse this to one page.
- FRICTION-0043: HIGH — Triage time is O(n): 28 min for 5 companies ≈ 5.6 min per company. For 15 companies, extrapolated: 84 minutes. Sam's success criteria: "under 15 minutes for 15 companies." Current: 6x over budget.
- FRICTION-0044: MEDIUM — Product switch is a form POST that sets a cookie and redirects. No client-side transition. Each switch costs 1-2 seconds of page load. Over 10 switches in a triage session, that's 10-20 seconds of blank screens.

## Verdict: SUCCESS (all products triaged) but deeply inefficient
## Would Recommend: No — "If the dashboard says Company C is green and I find out it missed payroll, we have a trust problem."
