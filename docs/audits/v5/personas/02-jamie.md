# Persona 02 — Jamie

## Identity

- **Role:** Two-product founder, learning fleet mechanics
- **Background:** Non-technical co-founder who built two complementary B2B tools — a scheduling widget and an analytics add-on — with a contract dev team. Understands product-market fit intuitively but relies on others for infrastructure decisions. Manages the business via spreadsheets and Notion.
- **Current situation:** Both products are live with paying customers (~$8K combined MRR). Jamie added the second product to Foundry last week and is confused by how the fleet view differs from the single-product dashboard. Wants to understand cross-company patterns but does not know what questions to ask.

| Attribute | Value |

| Technical comfort | 4/10 |

## Abandonment Triggers

1. Fleet view is visually identical to single-company view — no clear value from managing both in one place.
2. Terminology assumes engineering fluency (e.g., "remediation PR", "circuit breaker") without explanation.
3. Cannot figure out which product is healthier at a glance within 5 seconds of loading the dashboard.
