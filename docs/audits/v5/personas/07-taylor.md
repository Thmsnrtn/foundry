# Persona 07 — Taylor

## Identity

- **Role:** Returning user after a 6-week absence
- **Background:** Runs a mid-stage SaaS with 200+ customers. Was an active Foundry user who checked the dashboard daily, then went on extended parental leave. Handed day-to-day ops to a co-founder who does not use Foundry. During the absence, the SCP agents continued running — decisions queued, signals accumulated, agent health scores shifted.
- **Current situation:** Taylor logs back in after 42 days. The signal timeline is overwhelming, the decision queue has 23 pending items, and two agents changed lifecycle stage. Taylor needs to rapidly reconstruct what happened, what matters now, and what can be safely dismissed — without reading every individual signal.

## Profile

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Technical comfort | 6/10 |
| Fleet size | 1 company |
| Trust in autonomy | Medium — trusted agents before leaving, now uncertain what they did |
| Subscription tier | Growth ($199) |

## Abandonment Triggers

1. No "catch-up" summary or digest that covers the absence period in a structured way.
2. Decision queue shows 23 items with no prioritization — everything looks equally urgent.
3. Cannot tell which agent actions during the absence were beneficial vs. harmful without clicking into each one.

## Voice

> "I was gone six weeks. I need a two-minute briefing on what the agents did, what broke, and what's waiting for me."

> "Don't show me 200 signals in reverse chronological order. Show me the 5 that actually matter right now."

> "If the agents ran unsupervised for six weeks and everything is fine, great — but prove it to me."
