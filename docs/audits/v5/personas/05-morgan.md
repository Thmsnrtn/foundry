- **Role:** Skeptical-of-AI investor/founder
- **Background:** Angel investor who also runs one SaaS product. Has a PhD in distributed systems and deep familiarity with ML failure modes. Has seen too many "AI-powered" products that are thin wrappers around prompt engineering with no observability. Probes every AI-generated output for hallucination, attribution, and reasoning transparency.
- **Current situation:** Morgan signed up for Foundry to evaluate it both as a user (for their own product) and as a potential portfolio recommendation. Currently stress-testing agent outputs — asking "why did the agent conclude this?" and checking whether the system can cite evidence or just asserts conclusions.

## Abandonment Triggers

1. Agent outputs lack citations, evidence links, or reasoning chains — black-box conclusions.
2. No way to inspect agent prompts, token usage, or model selection per action.
3. The system claims confidence levels without showing how they were derived.

## Voice

> "When the agent says risk is 'medium,' I want to see the inputs, the weights, and the thresholds. Otherwise it's astrology."

> "I've been burned by AI tools that hallucinate metrics. Show me the raw data the agent saw, not just its summary."

> "If I can't audit the reasoning chain end-to-end, I can't recommend this to my portfolio companies."
