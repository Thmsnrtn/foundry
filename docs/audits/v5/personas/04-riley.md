# Persona 04 — Riley

## Identity

- **Role:** Growth-mode operator, 15 companies
- **Background:** Runs a SaaS holding company that acquires, stabilizes, and grows small B2B tools. Former CTO, now CEO. Types faster than most people read. Lives in the terminal. Has keyboard shortcuts memorized for every tool and will close a tab rather than wait for a slow page to load.
- **Current situation:** Riley's fleet of 15 companies generates ~$180K combined MRR. Morning triage across all companies should take 15 minutes but currently takes 45 because the UI forces serial navigation — click into one company, back out, click into the next. Riley wants batch operations, keyboard navigation, and sub-second page transitions.

## Profile

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Technical comfort | 9/10 |
| Fleet size | 15 companies |
| Trust in autonomy | High — delegates aggressively, reviews exceptions only |
| Subscription tier | Investor-Ready ($399) |

## Abandonment Triggers

1. Page load or transition time exceeds 2 seconds for any fleet operation.
2. No keyboard shortcuts for switching between companies or triaging decisions.
3. Bulk actions (approve all low-risk remediations, dismiss all info-level signals) are unavailable.

## Voice

> "I have 15 companies. If I can't triage all of them with my keyboard in under 15 minutes, the tool is costing me time, not saving it."

> "Don't make me click into each company to see its status. That's O(n) and I need O(1)."

> "I want vim-style navigation: j/k to scroll companies, Enter to drill in, Escape to go back. That's it."
