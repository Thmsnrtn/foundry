# Massachusetts Commercial Millwork Bid Brief — September 10, 2026 edition

**Pulled:** September 10, 2026, from the COMMBUYS open solicitations · **Covers:** bid notices open for bidding on that date, with an opening date still ahead, that look relevant to cabinetmaking, casework, countertops, carpentry or architectural millwork.

## What this is, and what it is not

This is a shortlist, not a database. Every item below links to the public notice on COMMBUYS, the Commonwealth of Massachusetts procurement record. Dates, agencies and contacts are copied from those notices; nothing here is estimated.

**Coverage is limited to COMMBUYS.** COMMBUYS listed 993 solicitations on the pull date. Of those, 726 were still open for bidding with an opening date in the future; every one of those was screened by title, and each candidate was read in full. This brief does **not** cover the Central Register (the Secretary of the Commonwealth's paid weekly listing of public construction), DCAMM's Bid Express e-bid room (login required), or municipal portals that do not post to COMMBUYS. Something that is not here is not necessarily absent from the market.

**Relevance is judged from the notice text.** We did not download the bid documents. Where a notice says "renovation", the presence of casework or millwork is a reasonable inference and nothing more — those entries say so in as many words. Always read the bid documents before spending time on a bid.

Items are listed by bid opening date. "Closing soon" means the opening is within seven days of this brief.

## Screened and excluded

Of the 726 notices open for bidding, 114 matched a building-related title and the plausible ones were read in full. Excluded after reading: roofing and cooling towers (DCAMM State Transportation Building); windows, siding and HVAC only (Groton Housing Authority); exterior doors only (Nashoba Regional School District; Montague Housing Authority; City of Worcester Senior Center entrance); overhead and garage doors (Westfield, Southborough, Somerville, Mansfield, Berlin); interior finishes with no millwork named — carpet, ceiling tile, sheetrock, painting (Brockton Trial Court); a qualification statement rather than a bid (Massport, Worcester Regional Airport); and everything outside the building trades — roads, bridges, water and sewer works, lighting, professional and design services, IT and audio-visual.

Also excluded: the City of Boston South End Branch Library contractor prequalification, which appeared in the September 7 edition of this brief. Its RFQ closed at noon on September 10 and it is not carried forward.

## How to use this

Read the bid documents at the source named in each notice — biddocs.com, Projectdoc.com, or the agency's own page. Several housing-authority projects state that bids are **not** submitted through COMMBUYS. Public projects over the M.G.L. c.149 thresholds require bid deposits and, where stated, DCAMM certification; the notices say so where it applies.
