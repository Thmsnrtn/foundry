## Qualified exposure

For the kill rule "no settled payment after 25 qualified exposures", a **qualified exposure** is one business, after owner review, that has been sent the outreach message and to which the message was delivered (no bounce). Web-form submissions count when the form confirms submission. Phone calls count only if the message was actually conveyed to a person who runs or estimates for the business.
