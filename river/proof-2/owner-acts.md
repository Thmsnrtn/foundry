# Experiment 002 — the owner's acts

Only what Thomas literally must do himself. Foundry cannot perform any of these; each is an external boundary the institution does not cross on his behalf.

## Before the listing goes live

1. **Approve the experiment in Foundry** on its own page. That seals the design, sets the $25 allowance for 30 days, and records that Foundry contacts nobody and publishes nothing for this test.
2. **Open the Etsy shop as Apex Micro** under your own identity: Persona ID check and selfie, name, date of birth, address, partial SSN, and the one-time non-refundable $15 set-up fee.
3. **Attach one bank account in your name that holds only Apex Micro money** to Etsy Payments, and let the 5-day hold pass before listing. The name on the account must match your ID.
4. **Opt out of Offsite Ads** in Shop Manager → Settings → Offsite Ads. Do not start an Etsy Ads campaign. Do not enrol in Share & Save.
5. **Read Etsy's Fees & Payments Policy and Creativity Standards on screen.** This environment could not fetch them; the design rests on what Etsy's own pages said through search.
6. **Review the workbook, the how-to, the listing text with its disclosures, and the shop privacy policy.** Open the workbook in Excel or Google Sheets and confirm the example rows compute (the build environment could not produce cached values, so the file is set to calculate fully on open); then publish the listing at $14 with the AI-assistance disclosure in the description and the "Designed by" attribution set. Set the shop privacy policy text in the shop's policies.
7. **Paste the listing URL into Foundry** on the experiment page. That places the exposure and starts the 30-day clock.

## During the window

8. **Enter the venue's readings** on the experiment page at day 7, 14 and 30: impressions and position from Search Analytics, views, visits, favourites, orders, and the traffic-source split from Stats.
9. **Enter each order** as it appears: Etsy order number, date, gross amount, fees from the Etsy Payments statement. Enter any refund or case.

## At settlement

10. Nothing. Foundry settles by the sealed rule when the window closes, and records what changes next.
