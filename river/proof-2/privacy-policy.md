# Apex Micro on Etsy — privacy policy

This policy covers what Apex Micro (Thomas Norton, Massachusetts) does with information when you buy from this Etsy shop. It sits alongside Etsy's own privacy policy, which governs Etsy's platform.

**What I receive.** When you buy, Etsy shows me your name, your Etsy username, and the email address Etsy provides for the order. For a digital download there is no shipping address.

**What I do with it.** I use it only to fulfil the order and to answer any message you send about it, through Etsy. I do not add you to a mailing list, I do not write to you about other products, and I do not share it with anyone.

**What I keep.** Nothing outside Etsy. Etsy keeps the order record on its platform under its own retention rules. My own records hold the order number and the amount, never your name or email.

**Your rights.** If you would like me to stop using your information for anything at all, message me through Etsy and I will. If you are in the EU or UK you have the rights the GDPR gives you, including access, correction and erasure of what I hold, which is the order number and amount above.

**Refunds and contact.** If the workbook is no use to you, you can have your money back: message me through Etsy. Questions are answered by a person.
