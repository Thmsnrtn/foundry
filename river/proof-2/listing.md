# Bid Decision Workbook for Small Contractors — Excel & Google Sheets

**Which bids are worth your time, and are you winning the ones you chase?**

One workbook for one decision: bid or don't. Log every bid you consider. The sheet works out, from your own history, how often you win from each lead source, what a bid is worth once you allow for that, and whether the hours it takes to bid are worth it. A summary shows win rate by lead source, why you lose, and how much submitted work is sitting against the capacity you set.

**What's in it**

- Bids sheet: one row per bid — client, scope, lead source, dates, estimated value, hours to bid, status, and why you lost. Drop-downs for lead source, status and loss reason.
- Computed for each bid: days to due, due flag, follow-up flag, the win rate used, expected value, bid cost, expected value per bidding hour, and a plain Signal: Worth it / Thin / Skip?
- Summary sheet: overall win rate, average value won vs lost, bidding hours per $1,000 won, submitted work against one month's capacity, win rate by lead source (with a chart), and a count of loss reasons.
- Settings sheet: your bidding hourly rate, monthly capacity, starting win-rate guess, and the lead sources and loss reasons you use. Yellow cells are yours to edit.
- Start-here sheet with a five-minute how-to and a legend.
- Example rows you delete before you start.

**What it is not**

It does not price or estimate a job, write proposals or invoices, schedule work, or track costs on a job. No macros, no add-ins, no account, nothing that phones home.
